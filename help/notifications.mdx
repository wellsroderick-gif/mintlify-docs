WR-Games keeps you in the loop through three notification channels: in-app, email, and SMS. Each channel serves a different purpose, and you can tune them independently so you get the updates that matter to you without the noise.

---

## In-App Notifications

In-app notifications are always on and cannot be disabled. The bell icon in the navigation bar shows your unread count. Tap it to view your full notification history.

Notification types delivered in-app include:

- **Roll Call prompts**: sent before an event to confirm you're still attending
- **Event reminders**: a nudge 24 hours before an event you're attending
- **RSVP activity**: who's RSVP'd to an event you're hosting, in one notification rather than one alert per person. It arrives within about a minute of a change. While it's unread, it keeps updating and moves to the top of your list. Once you read it, the next change starts a new one. The RSVP activity email is still a rollup, with updates more often as the event gets close
- **Waitlist confirmations**: when you join the waitlist for a full event
- **Group announcements**: messages from group organizers
- **Event cancellations**: when an event you've RSVP'd to is cancelled
- **Kudos received**: when another player sends you kudos
- **Review requests**: after an event you attended, asking how it went
- **Kudos requests**: after an event you hosted, asking you to give kudos to the people who came
- **Organizer handovers**: when a group's organizer role transfers to you
- **Leadership openings filled**: when an open leadership role in your group is taken
- **Friend requests**: when someone adds you as a friend
- **Favorite game alerts**: when games you've favorited are coming up at events near you

<Note>
  Several of these arrive by **email as well as the bell**: event reminders, waitlist confirmations, review and kudos requests, RSVP activity, organizer handovers, and leadership updates all land in both places. You can tune the email side in **Settings → Notifications**; the bell itself is always on.
</Note>

## Email Notifications

Email notifications are opt-out by default. You can manage all email preferences in **Settings → Notifications**.

### Weekly Digest

A weekly digest email lands in your inbox every Monday morning with a roundup of upcoming events from your groups. You can unsubscribe from the digest without affecting other email notifications.

The digest features up to four events, starting with ones you haven't RSVP'd to yet. On a quieter week, the rest fill with events you're already part of, each marked **You're Hosting**, **You're Going** or **You're Waitlisted**.

- Events you've said you're not going to never appear.
- A recurring event appears once, on its next date.

### Event Notifications

WR-Games sends event-specific emails for RSVPs, cancellations, and Roll Call prompts. These are tied to events you've joined or are hosting.

### Favorite Game Alerts

Favorite a game and WR-Games keeps watch for you. An upcoming event counts when one of your favorites is:

- the event's game,
- an expansion added to the event, or
- a game someone is bringing on the event's **On the Table** list.

WR-Games looks at events closest to you first. If there aren't any, it looks a little farther out, and then at online events. You're never told about the same event twice.

The email arrives with the subject **Your favorites are On the Table**, and the notification lands in your bell. **See All Events** opens the Events page showing every event with your matched games, so you can pick one.

#### How Often

Turn the email on or off in **Settings → Privacy** with **Favorite Game Notifications** ("Get an email when your favorite games are On the Table near you."). It starts on. **How Often**, right under it, decides how the alerts are spaced out.

| How Often | What You Get |
| --- | --- |
| **Once a Week** (where it starts) | At most one email and one notification a week, rounding up every new match since the last one. |
| **Every Time** | Each new event with one of your favorites gets its own email and notification, about a day after your game shows up on it. |

<Note>
  How Often only applies while **Favorite Game Notifications** is on. Turn it off and you get no favorite game emails at all, but the weekly notification in your bell carries on.
</Note>

## SMS Notifications

Text messages need a verified phone number. Once yours is verified, **SMS Notifications** and its categories start on, and you can turn any of them off. Texts are reserved for time-sensitive updates: Roll Call prompts, event reminders and cancellations.

<Note>
  During the beta, Roll Call texts are on for every group and for member-hosted events. Text messages are normally a paid feature, so this may change. **SMS Notifications** has to be on before any category below it will send.
</Note>

### Setting Up SMS Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Account">
    Open your settings and navigate to the **Account** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and Verify Your Phone Number">
    Enter your mobile number and complete the verification step. WR-Games will send a one-time code to confirm your number. Phone verification works with US and Canadian numbers only. If you ask for several codes in a short time, you'll have to wait before trying again.
  </Step>
  <Step title={"Go to Settings → Privacy & Visibility"}>
    Open the **Privacy & Visibility** tab in your settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the SMS Notifications Master Toggle">
    Turn on the **SMS Notifications** master switch. This must be on for any SMS to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Individual SMS Categories">
    Enable or disable specific categories:

    - **Roll Call**: confirmation prompts before events, and the text you get when you come off the waitlist
    - **Event Reminders**: reminders for upcoming events you've RSVP'd to
    - **Cancellations**: immediate alerts when an event is called off
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Opting Out of SMS

Reply **STOP** to any WR-Games text message to opt out of all product SMS immediately. Reply **START** to resume receiving messages. You can also toggle SMS off at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Visibility**.
