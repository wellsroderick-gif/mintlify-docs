WR-Games pulls board game groups in from Meetup so players can find games near them in one place instead of hunting across sites. If you run one of those groups, there's a good chance we already have a page for it, with your upcoming Meetup events on it.

Nobody's running that page yet. Claiming it brings your group onto WR-Games, and it takes about a minute.

<Note>
  **You don't have to leave Meetup.** Keep posting there, run everything on WR-Games, or do both. If you'd rather RSVPs stay where they are, pick link-out mode and WR-Games sends people to your Meetup event instead of collecting RSVPs itself.
</Note>

## How to Tell If a Group Is Unclaimed

An unclaimed group shows an **Unclaimed** badge on its page, near the group's location and where its events come from.

## Claiming Your Group

The quickest way is from the group's own page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Group's Page">
    Find your group on WR-Games and select **Claim this listing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm You're Authorized">
    The screen says **Claim this group**. Tick **I confirm I'm authorized to run this group**. **Continue** stays disabled until you do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Details and Pick Your RSVP Mode">
    [Meetup Integration](/groups/meetup-integration) covers the difference between link-out and side-by-side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim It">
    The page is yours from that moment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Claiming From a Pasted Meetup Link

You can also claim from the same place you'd import a group from Meetup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Import Option">
    Go to **Create a Group**. Above the form for a new group, you'll see the option to import from Meetup instead. If you're still setting up your account, the same option appears in the onboarding wizard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste Your Meetup Group URL">
    Enter your Meetup group's web address, exactly as you would to [import a group](/groups/import-from-meetup) for the first time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the Wizard Find Your Group">
    Rather than building a new group, the wizard tells you **We already have a page for this group** and shows you what it found: cover image, name, city, and how many upcoming events are already on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Details and Pick Your RSVP Mode">
    Same choices as a normal import. [Meetup Integration](/groups/meetup-integration) covers the difference between link-out and side-by-side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm You're Authorized">
    Select **I confirm I'm authorized to run this group**, then claim it. The page is yours from that moment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Nothing Resets When You Claim

Claiming takes over the page that's already there. It doesn't build a second one.

| What People Already Have | What Claiming Does to It |
| :-- | :-- |
| The link they've been sharing | Keeps working |
| Events already on the page | Stay exactly where they are |
| Upcoming events on Meetup | Keep syncing across |
| Search results pointing at the page | Keep pointing at it |

You also get the same tools as any other WR-Games group:

- **A full RSVP suite** with waitlists and pre-event Roll Call
- **Event notifications** by email and text
- **Custom roles and permissions**

<Tip>
  You can change your group's web address later if you want a different one. Claiming doesn't lock you in, and it doesn't break the link people already have.
</Tip>

## What You Get on WR-Games

- **Roll Call** for tracking who's actually coming
- **Search by game**, so players find your events through the games on the table
- **Event tools built for board games**

## Before You Claim

<Note>
  Claiming is for organizers listed on the group's Meetup page. If ownership is ever disputed, WR-Games will investigate and resolve it in favor of the authorized organizer.
</Note>

The confirmation box during the claim is where you tell us you're authorized to run the group.

## If You Already Run a Group on WR-Games

If you link Meetup from a group you already run, and WR-Games already has a page for that Meetup group, you're asked what to do with the page before anything is linked.

| Choice | What Happens |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Merge it into [your group]** | The page's Meetup events move to your group, the page leaves the directory, and its old link sends people to your group. |
| **Take over that page as its own group** | You run it as a separate group, and your current group stays as it is. You confirm you're authorized, as with any claim. |

Take over isn't always offered. If the page is still being reviewed, it can only be merged. If your plan has no room for another group, you'll need room first. The Free plan allows one.

If your group is hidden, the old page's link doesn't redirect, so your group's link stays private.

## If Someone Else Claimed Your Group

Get in touch and tell us which group it is. WR-Games can look into it, and once we've verified that you run the group, we can give you access.
