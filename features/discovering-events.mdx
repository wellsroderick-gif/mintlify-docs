The **Events** page lists upcoming board game events, with the ones near you first. Search for a game you want to play, narrow the list with filters, and save anything you want to come back to.

## Finding Events Near You

The page starts from your city and shows events there first. Events from other places sit below them under **Events farther away**. To look somewhere else, pick a city in the city box beside the search bar. If WR-Games doesn't know where you are yet, the page asks you to **choose your city to see local events**.

## Searching

The search bar finds events by **name, group, or game**. Type a game's title and you get the upcoming events playing it.

<Tip>
  You can also start from a game: its page on WR-Games lists the upcoming events playing it, with the ones near you first.
</Tip>

## Filtering

On a computer, the filters sit above the list. On a phone, select **Filters**, make your picks, then select the **Show** button, which counts the matching events. Filters combine, so you can ask for Open Game Nights this weekend, in person.

| Filter | Choices |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Date** | All Dates, Today, Tomorrow, This Week, This Weekend, Next Week, Next Weekend, or **Custom** for your own date range |
| **Event Type** | All Types, Game Session, Open Game Night, Custom Event |
| **Location** | All, In Person, Online |
| **Platform** | Where an online event is played |

To start over, clear your filters (**Clear all** on a computer, **Clear filters** on a phone). Not sure which event type you want? See [Event Types](/features/event-types).

## Sorting

Sort the list by **Upcoming**, **Newest**, **Trending** or **Nearest**. Signed in, you also get **Recommended**, which picks events for you. Each sort can also run in reverse.

## Discovering Events Through Groups

Once you belong to a group, the **Events** page shows that group's upcoming events whether or not you've RSVP'd. That includes events in private and hidden groups you're a member of.

| Where | What You See |
| --- | --- |
| On a phone | A toggle under the page title: **All Events** and **My Group Events**. WR-Games remembers the one you picked last, and filters and sorting work in either one. |
| On a computer | No toggle. **From Your Groups** comes first, then every other event. Nothing is listed twice. |

If your groups have nothing coming up, **My Group Events** tells you "No upcoming events in your groups right now." and offers an **All Events** button.

<Note>
  If you're signed out, or you haven't joined a group yet, the Events page shows the full list only.
</Note>

Not in a group yet? Open the **Groups** tab to find one near you. [How groups work](/concepts/groups).

## Where Online Events Show Up

An online event counts as local when its community is local. For a group's event, that's the group's location. For an event a member hosts, it's the host's profile city.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Events Page and Homepage">
    Local online events sit alongside the in-person events near you. Other online events appear under **Events farther away**. With the **Online** filter on, local online events come first.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="A Game's Page">
    Game Sessions near you come first, then **Online Game Sessions from other areas**, then **Events farther away**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

An online event's card says **Online** or shows the platform it's played on. It never shows a distance.

<Tip>
  Running a group? Set your group's city so its online events count as local for members nearby.
</Tip>

## Saving Events for Later

Not ready to RSVP? Open the event and select **Save** next to the RSVP button. It changes to **Saved**. Your saved events are on the **Saved** tab of **My Events**. Saving doesn't RSVP you or hold a spot. Select **Saved** again to take the event off your list.

## Hear About Games You Want to Play

Favorite a game, and WR-Games tells you when it's coming up at an event near you. Only favorites send these alerts, so favorite anything you're waiting to play, including games on your Want to Play list. See [Favorite Game Alerts](/help/notifications).
