Already keep your venue's schedule in Google Calendar, Outlook or another calendar app? You don't have to type it all into WR-Games again. The **Calendar Feed** tab in Manage brings it in for you, and gives you a link anyone can subscribe to.

If you haven't been in Manage before, start with [Run Your Venue](/features/running-your-venue).

## Who Can Use the Calendar Feed Tab

The owner, and anyone on the team whose role includes **Calendar Feed**. The **Manager** role has it to start. [Run Your Venue With a Team](/features/run-your-venue-with-a-team) covers roles.

## Importing Your Calendars

You can bring in more than one calendar, like your public events and a separate tournament calendar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy Your Calendar's Link">
    In your calendar app, find the calendar's iCal or webcal link. It usually starts with `https://` or `webcal://`, and calendar apps often list it under sharing or export settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add It in WR-Games">
    In Manage, open **Calendar Feed**. In the **Import Your Calendars** card, paste the link into **Calendar Link**. If you already have a calendar there, select **Add Calendar** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name It and Choose Who Sees It (Optional)">
    Give it a **Name** if you like. Select **Private** to show its events to your venue team only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save Calendar**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each calendar gets its own row, with its name, the website it comes from, when it last synced and how many events it brought in. If you leave **Name** blank, the row uses the calendar's own name, or its website if it doesn't have one. Only your venue team can see the links you paste.

### Who Sees Imported Events

| If the Calendar Is | Its Events on Your Venue's Calendar Tab |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Private** left off | Shown to everyone, signed in or not |
| **Private** selected | Shown to your venue team only, marked **Private** with a lock |

### Keeping Them Up to Date

WR-Games checks each calendar again every night. To pull in a change straight away, select **Sync Now** on that calendar's row. It syncs only that calendar.

If a calendar's nightly sync fails, its row says **Sync failed** and why, and the events from its last good sync keep showing. WR-Games tries again the next night. **Needs Attention** on the General tab names each calendar that didn't sync. Select **Fix Feed** to go straight to the Calendar Feed tab. A broken or moved link is the usual cause, so edit the calendar and paste a fresh one.

### Editing or Removing a Calendar

Open the **⋯** menu on a calendar's row.

- **Edit** changes its name, its link or **Private**. Select **Save Changes** when you're done. A new link replaces that calendar's imported events, and the form tells you how many before you save. Changing only the name or **Private** keeps them.
- **Remove** asks you to confirm, then **Remove Calendar** takes it off.

<Warning>
  Removing a calendar takes its imported events off your venue calendar. Your other calendars, and events members created at your venue on WR-Games, aren't affected.
</Warning>

## Sharing Your Subscribe Link

The **Subscribe Link** card gives you a link to your venue's events on WR-Games. Anyone who adds it to their calendar app sees your events there, and the calendar keeps itself up to date. Events imported from your own calendars aren't included.

- **Copy Link** copies it so you can share it on your website, in a newsletter or anywhere else.
- Members can find the same link themselves under **Subscribe** on your venue page's **Calendar** tab.

<Note>
  Google Calendar refreshes subscribed calendars every 12 to 24 hours, so a new event can take that long to show up for people using it.
</Note>

### Regenerating the Link

If your link has ended up somewhere you don't want it, select **Regenerate Link**. The old link stops working, and anyone who subscribed with it will need the new one.
