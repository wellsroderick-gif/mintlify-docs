Friends are the people you game with and want to keep track of. Everything happens from a member's profile.

## Send a Request

Open the member's profile and select **Add Friend**. The button changes to **Request Sent** while you wait for an answer.

## Changed Your Mind?

**Request Sent** isn't a dead end. Select it and confirm **Cancel friend request?** to take the request back. The profile goes back to **Add Friend**, so you can send a new one later.

## Answering a Request

When someone sends you a request, their profile shows buttons to accept or **Decline**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Accept">
    You're friends. The profile shows a **Friends** badge.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Decline">
    The request is turned down quietly. The other person isn't told. If you change your mind later, **Add Friend** is on their profile like anyone else's.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Removing a Friend

On their profile, open the menu beside the **Friends** badge and choose **Unfriend**.

## Your Friends List and Emails

Both are in **Settings → Privacy & Visibility**.

| Setting | What It Does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Show friends list on my profile** | Turn it off and visitors see a lock on your Friends tab instead of your list |
| **Friend Request Emails** | Emails when someone sends you a request, when one is waiting for you, and when someone accepts yours |

You'll also get an in-app notification when someone sends you a request. See [Notifications](/help/notifications).
