Messages holds two lists. **Conversations** are your direct messages with other members. **Inbox** is one-way mail from WR-Games and your groups, such as announcements and support replies.

## Finding Your Way Around

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="On a Computer">
    Your list stays on the left and the conversation you open fills the right. Nothing opens until you choose it, so a new message stays unread until you actually look at it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="On a Phone">
    You see the list first. Tap a conversation to open it full screen, and use the back arrow to return to the list.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

New messages show up in an open conversation, and in your list, within a couple of seconds. There's no need to refresh.

On the **Conversations** tab, three filters sit above the list: **All**, **Unread** and **Archived**.

## Archive a Conversation

Archiving tidies your list. It doesn't delete anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Conversation">
    Select it from your Conversations list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Menu">
    Select the **⋮** menu at the top of the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Archive Conversation">
    It leaves your main list and moves under **Archived**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Archiving only changes your list. The other person's copy stays exactly as it was, and they aren't told.

### Getting It Back

| What Happens | Result |
| :-- | :-- |
| Either of you sends a new message | It returns to your list on its own, as unread |
| You choose **Unarchive Conversation** from the **⋮** menu | It returns to your list |
| You open it from **Archived** or a link | You can read it, and it stays archived |

<Note>
  An archived conversation that's unread doesn't count toward your unread total. Archiving doesn't change email notifications, so if you'd rather not hear about a conversation at all, mute it instead.
</Note>

## Archive, Mute, or Block?

All three live in the same **⋮** menu and do different jobs.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Archive" icon="archive">
    Hides the conversation from your list. Comes back when there's a new message.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mute" icon="bell-off">
    Keeps it in your list but stops in-app notifications for it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Block" icon="ban">
    Stops messages between you, both ways.
  </Card>
</Columns>

To control who can start a conversation with you in the first place, see [Privacy Settings](/help/privacy-settings).
