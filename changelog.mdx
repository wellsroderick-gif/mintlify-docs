Here's what's new on WR-Games — newest first.

<Update label="August 2026" tags={["Events","Groups","Collections","Divvy","Notifications"]}>
  ## Rescheduling an event now re-notifies attendees

  When you move an event, we now notify attendees of the new time. A change of **24 hours or more** re-opens Roll Call for the new date, clearing earlier answers and asking everyone again — so your confirmed list reflects the time people are actually committing to. A change of **72 hours or more** re-sends the 24-hour reminders as well. Smaller adjustments stay quiet. [How Roll Call handles reschedules](https://help.wr-games.com/features/roll-call).

  ## Six more notifications now reach your bell

  Event reminders, waitlist confirmations, kudos requests, RSVP activity, organizer role updates, and leadership updates now appear in your notification bell alongside the email. If you don't watch your inbox closely, you'll still catch them. [Manage your notifications](https://help.wr-games.com/help/notifications).

  ## Clearer cancellation notices

  Everyone going or waitlisted gets the same clear notice when you cancel an event. Cancelling a series tells recipients the whole series has ended rather than naming a single date, and the **Not Going** link in a cancellation email now registers your response directly. [More on cancelling events](https://help.wr-games.com/features/creating-events).

  ## Your guests stay yours

  If you already have guests on an RSVP and the organizer later tightens the guest rules, your existing guests keep their spots. Guest allowances are also now set independently of other event settings, and the ceiling has moved up to 99 for large events.

  ## Handing off a recurring series gives you three choices

  When you transfer a series to a new host, you now decide what happens to your own place in it: stay on as a co-host, keep your seat as a regular attendee, or step away entirely. Previously this was a two-way toggle with no way to step down as host while keeping your spot.

  ## Changing a series cadence reschedules the events ahead of you

  Switch a series from weekly to every two weeks and the upcoming events now move to match. Before this, the cadence changed but the already-created events stayed where they were.

  ## Search BoardGameGeek straight from your collection

  My Collection's **Add Games** panel now searches BoardGameGeek, not just games already in the WR-Games directory. If a game isn't in our directory yet, you can still add it. You can also drop a game into several folders at once instead of adding it repeatedly.

  ## Bring a game from your group's shared collection

  The bring-a-game picker on an event now offers a third source, **Group Collection**, so you can browse what your group already owns collectively instead of hunting for it in the directory.

  ## Co-organizers can manage custom roles

  Creating and editing custom group roles is no longer organizer-only. Co-organizers can manage them too, which means role upkeep no longer waits on one person.

  ## Add a walk-in guest without leaving the picker

  Somebody turns up who isn't on the list and hands you a game. You can now create that walk-in guest directly from **Change who brought this** and **Change table lead**, instead of backing out to add them first.

  ## Divvy: set the player count, edit your own games

  Hosts can set minimum and maximum players for a Divvy session, and players can now edit or remove the games they added themselves rather than asking the host to do it.

  ## Friend requests behave the way you'd expect

  Two fixes. If someone sent you a request and you turned it down, adding them yourself later now makes you friends immediately instead of starting a fresh request. And you can cancel a request you've sent from the other person's profile, rather than having no way to take it back.

  ## Help links where you're actually stuck

  Contextual help links now sit next to the settings and controls they explain across the app, so the answer is one tap away instead of a search. The event page also collapses more neatly on mobile, putting the details that matter above the RSVP button.
</Update>

<Update label="July 2026" tags={["Events","Transit","RSVP"]}>
  ## Transit options on event pages

  In-person event pages in the DC area now show transit options — the **Getting there** card gives you the nearest Metro station or bus stop, walking time, and the lines or routes served, plus live train and bus arrivals on the day of the event (with first and last departures so you know you can still get home). It's public, so anyone can see how to reach a venue. [See how it works](https://help.wr-games.com/features/transit).

  ## Live arrivals stay readable at busy stations

  Transfer stations were showing so many departures that the card became noise. Arrivals are now capped to the next six, and you can tap a line to filter down to just that one. [See how it works](https://help.wr-games.com/features/transit).

  ## A fairer waitlist that won't split your group

  When a spot opens, the waitlist now scans past anyone whose party is too large to fit rather than stopping at the front of the queue, so seats don't sit empty. And nobody's group gets broken up to fill a partial opening: if your whole party can't come off the waitlist together, you keep your place until it can.

  ## Recurring series can end on a date you choose

  Series scheduling now takes an **End by** date, so a season or a limited run stops when you say it does instead of running on indefinitely.

  ## Editing a recurring series reaches every future event

  Choosing **this and all following** on a series edit now applies your change to all of the upcoming events, not just the ones already generated.

  ## Attendees are told when a host deletes their account

  If a host closes their WR-Games account, the people signed up to their events now hear about it instead of finding out when the event quietly disappears.

  ## Third-party tools and sites on /apps

  The **/apps** page now points to useful tools and sites built by other people in the board game community, alongside our own.
</Update>

<Update label="June 2026" tags={["Integrations","Calendar","Platform"]}>
  ## Connect your Meetup group to WR-Games

  Already running your group on Meetup? You can now connect it to WR-Games and bring your events over — sync them in to sit side by side with your WR-Games events so members can RSVP without leaving, or simply link out to Meetup. A quick **Refresh** pulls the latest whenever you cancel or update something on Meetup. [See how the Meetup integration works](https://help.wr-games.com/groups/meetup-integration).

  ## Your BoardGameGeek collection, fully under your control

  Syncing your BGG collection is faster and far more flexible. Your games show up almost immediately while the details fill in behind the scenes, you choose which collections to pull when you first connect, and you can reset or wipe your synced collection whenever you want. [Learn how BGG sync and your Collection work](https://help.wr-games.com/features/bgg-collection).

  ## Add custom entries to your group calendar

  Mark up your group's calendar with custom entries — birthdays, reminders, social nights, whatever your group needs. They save automatically, show up for everyone in the group, and flow into any subscribed calendar feed. [See group calendar custom entries](https://help.wr-games.com/groups/group-calendar-custom-entries).

  ## A new support center at help.wr-games.com

  We launched a support center — clear, searchable guides for groups, events, RSVPs, integrations, and more, so you can get an answer fast instead of guessing. This changelog lives here too. [Visit the support center](https://help.wr-games.com).
</Update>
