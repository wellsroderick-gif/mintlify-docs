You start a subscription in WR-Games. Everything after that — changing plans, switching to annual, cancelling, invoices, updating your card — happens in the **Stripe billing portal**, which WR-Games opens for you from your settings. We don't run billing in-house, so there's no second set of controls to hunt for.

## Finding Your Subscription Settings

Open **Settings** from the menu in the top right, then choose the **Subscription** tab.

The **Your Plan** card shows which plan you're on, what it costs, and what it includes. On an active paid plan you'll also see:

- **Next charge** — the amount and the date
- **Billing** — Monthly or Annual
- **Status** — active, past due, and so on

Below that, the **Event Credits** card shows your current balance and lets you buy more.

## Starting or Upgrading a Plan

This is the one billing action that happens inside WR-Games.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subscription tab">
    **Settings → Subscription**, then find the **Ready to level up?** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the plans">
    The two columns show your current plan next to the one above it. They're there to compare — the buttons underneath are what you click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick monthly or annual">
    **Upgrade to Standard (monthly)** or **Annual (save 20%)**. Pro works the same way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay through Stripe">
    You'll land on a secure Stripe checkout page. Once Stripe confirms the payment, your new plan appears on the Subscription tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **View all plans and pricing** on the same card opens the full comparison if you want to see everything side by side first.
</Tip>

## Annual vs. Monthly Billing

Annual plans are billed once a year at 20% off the monthly rate.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | You save |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Standard | \$10 / month | \$96 / year | 20% |
| Pro | \$20 / month | \$192 / year | 20% |

If you're already on an active monthly plan, the Subscription tab shows a **Switch to annual billing and save 20%** callout with a button carrying your exact total: **Switch to Annual — \$96/year** on Standard, **Switch to Annual — \$192/year** on Pro.

That button opens the Stripe billing portal straight to the plan-change screen. Once Stripe confirms the change, your plan and billing cycle update on the Subscription tab.

## Everything Else Happens in Stripe

Once you're subscribed, use **Manage subscription** on the Subscription tab — or the **Stripe billing portal** link next to **Cancel or change plans in the Stripe billing portal**. Either one opens the portal, where you can:

- Move to a different plan
- Cancel your subscription
- Download a PDF invoice for any past payment
- See upcoming charge dates and amounts
- Update your payment method or billing address

<Note>
  There's no invoice list on the Subscription tab. Receipts and invoices are in the portal.
</Note>

If your subscription lapses, a **Resubscribe** option appears on the tab so you can pick the same plan back up.

## If You Move to a Lower Plan

Your groups don't disappear, and you don't need to dismantle anything before you switch. Groups you already organize keep working past the limit. What changes is that you can't create another one until you're back at or below your new plan's cap.

| Your plan | Groups you can organize |
| :-- | :-- |
| Free | 1 |
| Standard | 2 |
| Pro | 5 |

The cap counts groups where you're the organizer. Stepping down as organizer of a group frees up a slot.

<Note>
  Billing is handled by **Stripe**. WR-Games never stores your full card details.
</Note>
