Here's what's new on WR-Games — newest first.

<Update label="September 2026" tags={["Groups","Calendar","Messages","Events"]}>
  ## Invite people directly to your group

  You can now invite a specific person to your group instead of only sharing a general join link. Pick a friend or an existing member, or enter an email address, and they get an invitation they can accept straight in. Need a link you can hand around? Create a shareable join link capped to a set number of uses. Event invitations got the same care: when someone accepts an invitation to one of your events, you get a notification instead of checking the event page.

  ## Your WR-Games calendar, everywhere

  Go to **Settings → Calendar** and turn on **Enable Calendar Feed** to get a private link. Subscribe to it once in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook and every event you're hosting or attending shows up there, across all your groups, and stays up to date on its own. Waitlisted events are off by default; switch them on and they appear as tentative. Each group's extra calendar entries can be turned off per group without touching the events you're attending. The link is yours alone, so keep it private. You can regenerate it or turn the feed off at any time.

  ## Messages: side-by-side view, archive, and live updates

  On desktop, Messages now keeps your conversation list visible while you read a thread. **Archive** a conversation from the thread menu, next to Mute and Block, to hide it from your list without deleting anything; it comes back on its own if either of you sends a new message. Open threads now update within a couple of seconds, and the Inbox loads in pages so it stays fast as it fills up.

  ## Change an event's type after it's published

  A published event is no longer locked to the type you picked when you created it. Open the event wizard and switch between Game Session, Open Game Night, and Custom Event. You'll see what the change affects before anything happens, and nothing changes until you save. Close the wizard without saving and the event stays exactly as it was. If a change would clear claimed tables or what attendees said they're bringing, you'll confirm first. [Compare the event types](/features/event-types).
</Update>

<Update label="August 2026" tags={["Events","Notifications","Groups","Collections","Divvy"]}>
  ## Rescheduling an event now re-notifies attendees

  When you move an event, we now notify attendees of the new time. A change of **24 hours or more** re-opens Roll Call for the new date, clearing earlier answers and asking everyone again — so your confirmed list reflects the time people are actually committing to. A change of **72 hours or more** re-sends the 24-hour reminders as well. Smaller adjustments stay quiet. [How Roll Call handles reschedules](/features/roll-call).

  ## Six more notifications now reach your bell

  Event reminders, waitlist confirmations, kudos requests, RSVP activity, organizer role updates, and leadership updates now appear in your notification bell alongside the email. If you don't watch your inbox closely, you'll still catch them. [Manage your notifications](/help/notifications).

  ## Clearer cancellation notices

  Everyone going or waitlisted gets the same clear notice when you cancel an event. Cancelling a series tells recipients the whole series has ended rather than naming a single date, and the **Not Going** link in a cancellation email now registers your response directly. [More on cancelling events](/features/creating-events).

  ## Three choices when you hand off a series

  Transferring a recurring series to a new host now asks what happens to your own place in it: stay on as a Co-Host, keep your seat as a regular attendee, or step away entirely. [How recurring series work](/features/recurring-series).

  ## Search BoardGameGeek from My Collection

  The Add Games panel in My Collection now searches BoardGameGeek, not just games already in the WR-Games directory, so you can add a game we don't have yet. You can also drop a game into several folders at once instead of adding it one folder at a time. [Managing your collection](/features/game-collection).

  ## Bring a game from your group's shared collection

  The bring-a-game picker on an event now offers a third source, **Group Collection**, alongside My Collection and the Directory, so you can pick from what your group already owns together. [How bringing lists work](/features/bringing-list).

  ## Co-organizers can manage custom roles

  Creating and editing custom group roles is no longer organizer-only. Co-organizers can manage them too, so role upkeep doesn't wait on one person. [Roles and permissions](/features/roles-and-permissions).

  ## Add a walk-in guest without leaving the picker

  Someone shows up who isn't on the list and hands you a game. You can now create that walk-in guest directly from **Change who brought this** and **Change table lead**, instead of backing out to add them first. [What's on the table](/features/on-the-table).

  ## Divvy: set player bounds, edit your own games

  Hosts can set minimum and maximum players for a Divvy session, and players can edit or remove the games they added themselves rather than asking the host to do it.

  ## Help where you're stuck, and a tidier event page on mobile

  Contextual help links now sit next to the settings and controls they explain across the app, so the answer is one tap away instead of a search. The event page also collapses more neatly on mobile, with the details that matter above the RSVP button. [Browse the help center](/).

  ## Every cancellation reaches you

  If an event you're signed up for is cancelled, you get a clear notice, including when WR-Games has to cancel it on the host's behalf. No more finding out because the event quietly disappeared. [Manage your notifications](/help/notifications).

  ## Group Game Sessions suggest a capacity

  Create a group Game Session, pick a game, and the capacity field now starts with that game's max player count, including any expansions you selected. It's a suggestion, not a rule: change it, clear it for an unlimited event, or raise it if you're running several tables. Existing events aren't touched. [Creating an event](/features/creating-events).

  ## Tools and sites we recommend on /apps

  The /apps page now has a section pointing to useful tools built by other teams, starting with Shared Game Timer and Board Game Video. They're recommendations, not integrations; nothing connects or syncs. [See the /apps page](https://wr-games.com/apps).
</Update>

<Update label="July 2026" tags={["Events","Transit"]}>
  ## Transit options on event pages

  In-person event pages in the DC area now show transit options — the **Getting there** card gives you the nearest Metro station or bus stop, walking time, and the lines or routes served, plus live train and bus arrivals on the day of the event (with first and last departures so you know you can still get home). It's public, so anyone can see how to reach a venue. [See how it works](/features/transit).
</Update>

<Update label="June 2026" tags={["Integrations","Calendar","Platform"]}>
  ## Connect your Meetup group to WR-Games

  Already running your group on Meetup? You can now connect it to WR-Games and bring your events over — sync them in to sit side by side with your WR-Games events so members can RSVP without leaving, or simply link out to Meetup. A quick **Refresh** pulls the latest whenever you cancel or update something on Meetup. [See how the Meetup integration works](/groups/meetup-integration).

  ## Your BoardGameGeek collection, fully under your control

  Syncing your BGG collection is faster and far more flexible. Your games show up almost immediately while the details fill in behind the scenes, you choose which collections to pull when you first connect, and you can reset or wipe your synced collection whenever you want. [Learn how BGG sync and your Collection work](/features/bgg-collection).

  ## Add custom entries to your group calendar

  Mark up your group's calendar with custom entries — birthdays, reminders, social nights, whatever your group needs. They save automatically, show up for everyone in the group, and flow into any subscribed calendar feed. [See group calendar custom entries](/groups/group-calendar-custom-entries).

  ## A new support center at help.wr-games.com

  We launched a support center — clear, searchable guides for groups, events, RSVPs, integrations, and more, so you can get an answer fast instead of guessing. This changelog lives here too. [Visit the support center](/).
</Update>
