WR-Games is built by a small team, and support isn't outsourced — a real person reads every message you send. Requests are tracked so nothing gets lost in a pile, and you can follow yours from start to finish.

<Tip>
  Before reaching out, check [Common Issues](/help/common-issues) — sign-in trouble, BGG sync, RSVPs, and notifications are all covered there with step-by-step fixes.
</Tip>

---

## Which option to use

There are three ways to reach us. Pick based on where you are and what's wrong:

| Your situation | Use this |
| --- | --- |
| Signed in, and you have a question or a problem with your account | **Support request** from your inbox |
| Signed in, and something on the site looks broken | **Bug reporter** |
| Signed out, or locked out of your account | **Contact form** |

<Note>
  Opening a support request from your inbox requires being signed in. If you can't get into your account, use the [contact form](https://wr-games.com/contact) instead — it works whether or not you're signed in.
</Note>

---

## Support request from your inbox

The best option for most questions, because your request and every reply stay together in one thread you can return to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your inbox">
    Go to **Messages** and select the **Inbox** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a request">
    Click **New support request** in the toolbar above your message list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Give it a short title and fill in the details. There's no category to pick — just tell us what's going on in your own words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Your new case opens in your inbox, and that's where replies will appear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bug reporter

If something on the site is visibly broken — a button that does nothing, a page that won't load, a number that looks wrong — use the bug reporter instead. It's the floating button available on any page while you're signed in.

The difference from a support request is that the bug reporter asks you to categorize what broke, which helps the report reach the right place faster. Both land with the same team.

## Contact form

The form at [**wr-games.com/contact**](https://wr-games.com/contact) is open to everyone, signed in or not. It's the right choice when you can't reach your account, and the only option if you don't have one.

<Warning>
  A message sent through the contact form won't appear in your WR-Games inbox, and won't have a case number to follow. Replies come by email instead. If you're signed in and want the thread in one place, open a support request from your inbox.
</Warning>

## Following your case

Once you've opened a support request, it lives in your inbox with a case number (something like `CASE-10842`) at the top of the thread. That number is worth quoting if you ever need to refer back to it.

Each case shows a status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | We've got it, and haven't started yet |
| **In progress** | Someone is actively working on it |
| **Resolved** | We believe it's sorted |
| **Closed** | The case is finished and no longer open for replies |
| **Dismissed** | No action was needed — often a duplicate |

You'll get a notification and an email whenever we reply, so you don't need to keep checking.

<Note>
  You can reply in the thread while a case is open. Once it moves to **Closed** or **Dismissed**, the reply box is replaced with "This case is closed." That's expected, not a bug — if the problem comes back, open a new request and mention the old case number.
</Note>

## What to include

The more context you give, the faster we can help.

<Columns cols={1}>
  <Card title="Your display name or account email" icon="user">
    Helps us find your account quickly, especially for access or billing questions.
  </Card>

  <Card title="The group or event you're asking about" icon="calendar">
    Include the name and, if you can, a direct link.
  </Card>

  <Card title="What you were trying to do" icon="message-square-text">
    What happened instead, and anything the error message said.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Note>
  If you're locked out of your account, put your **account email address** in the message itself — we can't look you up from the contact form otherwise.
</Note>

## Response times

We aim to reply promptly, but the team is small. Expect a few business days for most questions. Account access problems get prioritized.

There's no phone line and no live chat. Everything runs through the three options above, which is what lets every message get a considered reply rather than a canned one.
