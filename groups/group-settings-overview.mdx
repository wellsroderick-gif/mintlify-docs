Your group's settings live behind the gear icon on the group's page. This page walks through each section, so you know where to look before you go hunting.

What you see depends on your role. Organizers and Co-Organizers see the group's settings. Every member can reach **My Preferences**.

## Finding a Setting

Type in **Search settings** at the top. It finds sections, cards and individual settings (try "join questions" or "frequency") and takes you straight there.

Beside your group's name, **View Group Page** takes you back to the group.

## Overview

The first section is a quick health check on your group.

| Card | What It Shows |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Finish Setting Up Your Group** | A short checklist: add a group image, write a description, add a social link. **Set Up** beside an item takes you to it. |
| **At a Glance** | Your member count, plan, visibility, and whether joining is open or needs approval |
| Analytics summary | A snapshot of your group's numbers. <Badge color="orange" size="sm">Standard</Badge> <Badge color="orange" size="sm">Pro</Badge> |

The checklist disappears once all three items are done. It comes back if one of them is later removed.

<Note>
  **Imported your group from Meetup?** You may see a note saying your experience level, group type and frequency were set to standard values. **Update Details** takes you to them. Closing the note hides it for everyone who manages the group.
</Note>

## Privacy & Access

Who can see your group and how people get in: visibility, invitations, whether joining is open or needs approval, and join questions. [Group Privacy](/features/group-privacy) covers all of it.

## Features

The apps and tools your group uses.

- **Apps**: turn Chat, the Forum, Polls, Schedule Finder and the others on or off. When an app is off, its tab is hidden for everyone. See [Announcements and Communication Tools](/features/group-announcements).
- **New event notifications**: whether members are told automatically when a new event is published.
- **Forum settings**: appears once the Forum is on.
- **Meetup**: connect your group to Meetup. See [Meetup Integration](/groups/meetup-integration).

## My Preferences

These are yours alone. They don't change anything for other members.

| Card | What You Can Do |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Email Preferences** | Two separate switches: announcement emails and new event emails from this group. Turning one off stops only the email. In-app notifications still arrive. |
| **Play Tracking** | **Opt Out of Play Tracking for This Group**. When it's on, plays logged in this group won't link to your play history or notify you. |
| **Leave Group** | Leave the group. The Organizer doesn't see this, because an Organizer steps down instead. |

<Tip>
  The play-tracking switch covers this group only. To opt out everywhere, use the opt-out in your account **Settings**, under **Privacy & Visibility**.
</Tip>

<Info>
  **Play tracking is in beta.** The switch is there for every member, but you may not see play tracking itself in your group yet.
</Info>

## Danger Zone

Only the Organizer sees these two choices.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Step Down as Organizer" icon="log-out" href="/groups/leadership-transfers">
    Hand the group on. Co-Organizers are offered the role first, then all members.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Delete Group Permanently" icon="trash-2">
    Removes the group for good.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Danger>
  Deleting a group removes all its members, events and data, and it can't be undone.
</Danger>

If you're the only member, there's no one to hand the group to, so deleting is the only choice offered.

Members sometimes see a **Leadership & Transfer** card here instead. It appears when the Organizer hasn't visited the group in a long while. [Stepping Down and Taking Over](/groups/leadership-transfers) explains how that works.
