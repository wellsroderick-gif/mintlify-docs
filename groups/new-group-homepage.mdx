Your group's homepage is getting a new look: a grid of widgets you can arrange yourself. Until January 1, 2027, Organizers can decide when to switch, and switch back if it isn't working. On January 1, every group moves to the new homepage.

## What's New

The new homepage is a grid of widgets. Each widget shows one thing, like your next event, your game collection or your links. Members and visitors all see the same grid.

| To do this | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Move, resize, remove or add widgets | **Edit Homepage** |
| Change a widget's settings, like which event it spotlights | The gear on the widget, inside **Edit Homepage** |
| Pin an announcement to the homepage | **Send Announcement** |
| Choose which tabs show, and in what order | **Edit Menu**, at the end of the tab bar |

On a phone, your tabs sit in a dock at the bottom of the screen. To reorder them, use **Edit Menu** on a computer.

## Who Can Switch

Anyone whose role lets them manage the group's settings can switch, which by default means Organizers and Co-Organizers. Everyone else sees whichever homepage the group is using.

The choice is for the whole group. When you switch, every member and every visitor sees the change, including people who aren't signed in.

## Trying It for the First Time

The first time you open your group's page, you'll see a window titled **The New Homepage Is Here** with three buttons:

| Button | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Try It Now** | Your group switches to the new homepage for everyone, and a short tour shows you around. |
| **Not Yet** | Your group keeps the classic homepage. A pointer on the Settings gear shows where to switch later. |
| **Learn More** | Opens this page. |

Closing the window counts as **Not Yet**.

The tour walks through the grid, **Edit Homepage**, adding and setting up widgets, pinning an announcement and your tabs. You'll only see it once.

## Switching On or Back

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Group's Settings">
    On your group's page, select the gear icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Group Homepage">
    The **Group Homepage** card is at the top of the group's profile settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flip the Switch">
    Turn **Use the New Homepage** on or off. The change happens right away, with no need to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you switch back, we'll ask **What Didn't Work?** You can pick any reasons that fit, add a note, or skip it and select **Switch Back**. Your answers help us improve the new homepage before everyone moves to it.

## On January 1, 2027

<Info>
  On January 1, 2027, every group moves to the new homepage and the **Use the New Homepage** setting goes away. If your group is still on the classic homepage on December 2, 2026, you'll see a one-time reminder that a month is left.
</Info>

## If You Don't See the Choice

Some groups start on the new homepage and never see the window or the switch:

- Groups created after the new homepage launched
- Meetup listings claimed after the launch
- Groups where nobody who manages the settings had used WR-Games in the month before the launch

These groups get everything above except the switch.
