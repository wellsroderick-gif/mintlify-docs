WR-Games lets you report something that breaks the rules of the community. A report goes to the moderation team to review.

## What You Can Report

You can report an event, a group, a member, a game, or a venue. Each one has its own list of reasons.

| What You're Reporting | Reasons You Can Pick |
| :-- | :-- |
| Event | Safety concern, Harassment or bullying, Fraudulent or misleading listing, Host misconduct, Spam or scam, Other |
| Group | Harassment or bullying, Hate speech or discrimination, Impersonation or fake profile, Spam or scam, Misleading information, Other |
| Member | Harassment or bullying, Impersonation or fake profile, Spam or scam, Inappropriate behavior, Other |
| Game | Inaccurate information, Spam or scam, Duplicate listing, Other |
| Venue | Inaccurate information, Safety concern, Spam or scam, Other |

<Info>
  Reports are for content and behavior. If something on the site is broken, send a bug report. For a question or a problem with your account, send a support request. Both are covered on the [Contact](/help/contact) page.
</Info>

## How to Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Flag">
    Open the page for what you're reporting and select the flag icon. On a group, member profile, game, or venue page it sits with the page's other actions, and its label reads **Report this** followed by the type. You need to be signed in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Reason">
    Choose the reason that fits best. It's required, and the list depends on what you're reporting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Details">
    Describe what happened, including dates or links if they help. Details are optional, except when you pick **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit It">
    Select **Submit report**. You'll see your reference number, which looks like **RPT-00213**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens Next

The moderation team reviews your report. Your report is confidential, and only moderators can see it. The person you reported is never told who reported them.

When the team has reviewed it, you get an email with the result:

| Result | What It Means |
| :-- | :-- |
| Resolved | The team took action |
| Dismissed | No action was needed |

Thank you for helping keep the community welcoming. We'll stay in touch.

If the problem continues, submit a new report and mention the earlier reference number.
