Looking for a game that seats five and wraps up in an hour? The filters get you there. There are five of them, **Players**, **Weight**, **Rating**, **Playtime** and **Expansions**, plus **Owner** when you're looking at a group's games, and they work the same everywhere you see them:

- The [game picker](/features/game-picker), on every tab it has. **Pick Cover Art** is the one picker without filters, since you're only choosing box art there.
- The Games Directory page.
- Your group's **Collection** tab.

A game shows up only when it passes every filter you've set. Leave a filter alone and it lets everything through.

## Where the Filters Sit

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="On a Computer">
    The filters sit in a row under the search box. Click one to open it. The list updates as you pick, so there's nothing to apply. A filter you've set shows its choice on the button, like **2–4 Players** or **Weight 2.0–3.5**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="On a Phone">
    Tap **Filters** next to the search box. A number on the button counts how many filters you've set. They all open in one panel. When you're done, tap the button at the bottom. It tells you how many games match, as in **Show 12 Games**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Players

Players is a strip of numbers: **Any**, **1** through **9**, and **10+**. Tap one number to pick a count. Tap a second number to pick everything between the two.

| What You Tap | What You Get |
| --- | --- |
| One number | That player count. Picking 1 reads **Solo**. |
| A second, different number | A range, like 2 to 4. |
| Any number while a range is set | Starts over with just that number. |
| The number you already picked, or **Any** | Back to any player count. |

**10+** means 10 or more players.

### One Player Count

Pick one count and you see every game that can be played with that many people. Pick 4, and a game for 2 to 6 players shows up.

Turn on **Match Player Count Exactly** to narrow it to games made for that count and nothing else. With 1 picked and the switch on, you see only games built for one player. Wingspan plays 1 to 5, so it drops out.

### A Player Range: Any, All or Exactly

Pick a range of player counts and three choices appear under **Match**. They decide how closely a game's player count has to fit your range.

| Match | A Game Shows Up When |
| --- | --- |
| **Any** | It can be played with at least one of the player counts in your range. |
| **All** | It can be played with every player count in your range. |
| **Exactly** | Its player count is your range exactly, no more and no fewer. |

Here's how it works out with 2 to 4 players picked:

| Game | Players | Any | All | Exactly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Patchwork | 2 | Shown | Hidden | Hidden |
| Catan | 3 to 4 | Shown | Hidden | Hidden |
| Azul | 2 to 4 | Shown | Shown | Shown |
| Wingspan | 1 to 5 | Shown | Shown | Hidden |
| A game for 5 to 8 | 5 to 8 | Hidden | Hidden | Hidden |

<Tip>
  Hosting and not sure who'll turn up? Pick the range you're expecting and choose **All**. Every game left in the list works whether three people come or five.
</Tip>

## Weight and Rating

Both are sliders with two handles. Drag them to set the lowest and highest you want.

- **Weight** runs from 1.0 (Light) to 5.0 (Heavy), in half steps. It's BoardGameGeek's measure of how complex a game is.
- **Rating** runs from 1 to 10, in half steps. It's the game's BoardGameGeek rating.

Pull both handles back to the ends and the filter is off again.

## Playtime

Playtime is a list of eight lengths: **Under 15 min**, **15–30 min**, **30–45 min**, **45–60 min**, **60–90 min**, **90–120 min**, **2–3 Hours** and **Over 3 Hours**. Pick as many as you like, and a game shows up if its playtime falls in any of them.

Pick lengths that sit next to each other and they read as one span, so 30–45, 45–60 and 60–90 show as **30–90 min**. A game that runs exactly 60 minutes counts as 45–60 min.

## Expansions

**With Expansions** is where it starts, and lists base games and expansions together. Choose **Base Games Only** to hide the expansions.

<Note>
  A game with no weight, rating or playtime on record is hidden while you're filtering by that thing. If a game you expected is missing, clear that one filter and look again.
</Note>

## Owner

You'll find **Owner** on a group's **Collection** tab, and on the **Group Collection** tab when you add a game to On the Table or start a table. Pick a member to see only the games they own. On the group's **Collection** tab you can pick several. You're at the top of the list, under your own name.

On a computer, **Owner** is the last pill in the row. On a phone, it's the first section of the **Filters** panel, and it reads **Anyone** until you pick someone. The **Your Copies** view on the **Collection** tab has no Owner filter, since every game there is yours.

## Starting Over

**Reset Filters** clears them all at once. It only shows up once you've set something.

| Where You Are | What It Looks Like |
| --- | --- |
| A game picker, on a computer | A round-arrow button at the end of the filter row. Point at it to see its name. |
| The Games Directory page or your group's Collection tab, on a computer | A **Reset Filters** button at the end of the filter row. |
| Anywhere, on a phone | **Reset Filters** at the bottom of the Filters panel. |

In a game picker, your filters stay set while you switch between tabs, and they all clear when the picker closes.
