WR-Games gives you fine-grained control over what's visible on your public profile and how other members can interact with you. Privacy settings are found in **Settings → Privacy & Visibility** and apply to your public-facing profile unless otherwise noted.

---

## Profile Visibility

These settings control which reputation and social signals are shown on your public profile.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Trust Score" icon="shield-check">
    Show or hide your Trust Score on your public profile. Other players use your Trust Score to gauge reliability when you join new groups or events.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Reputation" icon="star">
    Show or hide your Reputation points (RP) and level badge on your public profile.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Friends List" icon="users">
    Choose whether your friends list is visible to anyone who views your profile, or keep it private.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Activity Streak" icon="flame">
    Show or hide your current activity streak on your public profile.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
  Even with your Trust Score hidden from your public profile, organizers in groups you belong to can still see it when reviewing join requests on **Standard\+** groups.
</Tip>

## Play Data

Control how other people's plays connect to you, and what you see in your own play history.

### Opt Out of Play Tracking

When someone else logs or imports a play with you in it, that play links to your play history and you're notified. Opting out stops both. You're still named on their play. It just doesn't link to you or notify you.

There are two switches, and they cover different ground:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Everywhere">
    **Opt Out of Play Tracking**, in **Settings → Privacy & Visibility**. Covers every group, plus plays people import from other apps. It's off to start with, so plays link to you until you turn it on.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="One Group">
    **Opt Out of Play Tracking for This Group**, in that group's settings under **My Preferences**. Covers plays logged in that group only, not imports. See [Group Settings](/groups/group-settings-overview#my-preferences).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
  **Play tracking is in beta**, so you may not see it everywhere yet.
</Info>

### Hide Scores in My Play History

Hides the numbers when you look at your own play history. Your scores are still saved and still count in your stats.

## Messaging

**Who can message you?** decides who can start a new conversation with you.

| Choice | Who Can Message You |
| --- | --- |
| **Anyone (registered members)** | Any signed-in WR-Games member |
| **Group members only** | Only members of groups you share |
| **Nobody (new conversations)** | No one can start a new conversation |

Choosing **Nobody** doesn't close conversations you already have. You can still read and reply to those. See [Messages](/features/messages) for archiving, muting and blocking.

## Collection Visibility

Turn on **Public Collection** and your profile gets a **Collection** tab other members can open. Turn it off and the tab is gone for them. You always see your own collection on your own profile, whatever you choose here.

When it's on, **Which collection appears on your profile** decides what shows:

| Choice | What Visitors See |
| --- | --- |
| **Owned games (default)** | Only the games marked owned in your library |
| A folder | Every game in that folder |

<Note>
  Only public folders can be shown. A private folder never appears in the list. To feature one, set it to public from **Manage collections** on your Collection page.
</Note>

Sharing folders with a group is a separate setting, also on your Collection page. See [Game Collection](/features/game-collection).

## Analytics and Cookies

WR-Games uses cookies for signing in and for product analytics and session recording, which help us see what's working and fix what isn't.

The first time you visit, a banner asks what you'd like:

| Choice | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Got It** | Keeps analytics and session recording on |
| **Decline** | Turns them off |

Change your mind any time with **Product analytics & session recording**, under **Analytics & tracking** in **Settings → Privacy & Visibility**.

<Tip>
  This choice is saved in the browser you make it in. On another browser or device, set it there too.
</Tip>

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal and you haven't chosen either way, analytics stay off. An explicit choice in the banner or in Settings always wins. The [Cookie Policy](https://wr-games.com/cookies) has the details.

## Event Page Modules

In-person event pages can show a weather forecast and nearby transit. If you'd rather not see them, turn them off for yourself under **Event Page Modules**.

| Switch | What Turning It Off Hides |
| --- | --- |
| **Show weather on event pages** | The forecast card and weather alerts, on every event page |
| **Show transit on event pages** | The transit card. See [Transit](/features/transit) for what it shows. |

<Tip>
  The X on a weather alert only hides it for that visit. These switches hide the module on every event until you turn them back on.
</Tip>

## Groups

You can show or hide your group memberships on your public profile. When hidden, other members visiting your profile will not see a list of the groups you belong to.

<Note>
  Privacy settings apply to your public profile page. Event attendee lists and group member directories are governed by each individual group's own settings, which are controlled by group organizers.
</Note>
