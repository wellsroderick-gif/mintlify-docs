Every member of a group has a role, and the role decides what they can do there. Every group comes with five roles. On Standard and Pro plans you can also change what each role can do, rename roles, and create your own.

## The Five Roles

| Role | Who It's For |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Organizer** | The person who runs the group. Can do everything. Each group has one. |
| **Co-Organizer** | Runs the group alongside the Organizer, with almost the same access. |
| **Moderator** | Keeps the forum, chat and group collection in order. |
| **Event Planner** | Creates and runs events. |
| **Member** | Everyone else: RSVPs, plays, and joins the conversation. |

Whoever creates a group becomes its Organizer. The Organizer role isn't assigned like the others. It moves from one person to another through a transfer. See [Stepping Down and Taking Over as Organizer](/groups/leadership-transfers).

Two more abilities belong to Co-Organizers and can't be switched off or handed to other roles: managing the group's settings, and creating, editing and deleting custom roles.

### Two Permissions That Sound Alike

| Permission | What It Covers |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Moderate content** | The forum and chat: editing or deleting other people's posts and moderating messages |
| **Manage group collection** | The group's game collection: assigning games to members, removing a member's copy, and adding games when members can't |

See [Adding Games to Your Collections](/features/adding-games) for what assigning games looks like.

## What Only the Organizer Can Do

A Co-Organizer can do almost everything the Organizer can. These stay with the Organizer:

- **Delete the group.**
- **Make someone a Co-Organizer**, or change a Co-Organizer to another role.
- **Ban a Co-Organizer.**
- **Manage a custom role with Co-Organizer access**: create, edit or delete one, or give it to or take it from a member, because each of those makes or unmakes a Co-Organizer.

## Changing What Each Role Can Do <Badge color="blue" shape="pill" icon="lock">Standard and Pro</Badge>

On Standard and Pro plans, open your group's settings from the gear icon and choose **Roles**. The **Role Permissions** card has a switch for every permission under each role.

- **Change a permission:** flip its switch, then select **Save Permissions**.
- **Rename a role:** select the pencil next to its name. The new name is what your group sees.
- **Start over:** **Reset to Defaults** puts every switch back to the settings above.

Co-Organizers can use the role editor too, with one limit: if you're a Co-Organizer, the Co-Organizer column is read-only for you. The Organizer can still change it.

## Custom Roles <Badge color="blue" shape="pill" icon="lock">Standard and Pro</Badge>

When none of the five roles fits, make your own, such as a Tournament Host or a Welcome Team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Roles">
    In your group's settings, choose **Roles** and select **Create Role** under **Custom Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name It and Pick a Starting Point">
    Give the role a name, then choose a role under **Start From Template** to copy its switches as a starting point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose What Happens on a Downgrade">
    **On plan downgrade, members become** sets the role these members move to if the group leaves Standard or Pro.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Permissions and Save">
    Turn individual permissions on or off, then select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once saved, the role can be given to members like any other. To change a custom role later, select **Edit** beside it.

<Warning>
  **Deleting a custom role can't be undone.** Members who had it go back to the role picked under **On plan downgrade, members become**.
</Warning>

The Organizer, Co-Organizers, and members whose custom role carries Co-Organizer access can all create, edit and delete custom roles. A custom role that carries Co-Organizer access is the exception, and only the Organizer can manage it.

## Assigning Roles

Anyone whose role includes **Promote / demote roles** can change a member's role. By default that's the Organizer and Co-Organizers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Members Tab">
    Go to your group and open its **Members** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Member's Menu">
    Find the member and select the **⋮** menu beside their name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Role">
    Choose **Change role**, then pick a role. Custom roles are listed too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choosing a role opens a confirmation window with **Notify Them**, which sends the member an email and a notification, and **Change Quietly**, which sends them nothing. The change takes effect right away. To change several members at once, use **Select** on the Members tab, pick the members, and choose **Change Role**.
