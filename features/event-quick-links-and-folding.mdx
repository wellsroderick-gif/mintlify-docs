On a phone, every event page has an **Event Quick Links** bar that gets you to any part of the event in two taps. If you're going to an event, you can also fold its details away so the page opens straight on **On the Table**.

---

## Event Quick Links

The **Event Quick Links** bar sits under the top navigation on every event page and stays put while you scroll. Tap it and a sheet opens with:

- **A row for each part of the event**, like **On the Table**, **Attendees** and **Discussion**. You only see the parts that event has. Tap one and the page takes you there.
- **Top of Page**, to jump back up.

The bar is on phones only. On a computer, the event's tabs are already in view.

### If You Can Manage the Event

Hosts, Co-Hosts and group members whose role lets them manage events see a **Hosting** group at the bottom of the sheet, in place of **Top of Page**. It has the same actions as the event's **Manage** menu, like **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Cancel event**, plus **Roll Call** while Roll Call is open.

---

## Fold Event Details When You're Going

The fold lives in **Event Page Modules**, beside the weather and transit switches. It's off until you turn it on. With it on, an upcoming event you're going to opens on **On the Table** on your phone, and the event details fold into a strip under the Event Quick Links bar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Settings">
    Go to **Settings** and choose the **Privacy & Visibility** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find Event Page Modules">
    Scroll to the **Event Page Modules** section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn On the Fold">
    Switch on **Fold event details when I'm going**. A **Fold Style** row appears under it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a Fold Style">
    Choose **Light** or **Extended**. Light is where it starts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The fold only applies when your RSVP is **Going** and the event hasn't ended or been cancelled. Waitlisted, or looking at a past event? You see the whole page as usual.

### Light or Extended

| What's Showing | Light | Extended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event image | Stays | Folds away |
| Name, time and where it is | Stays | Stays, on one line |
| Your RSVP | A **You're Going** button that opens your RSVP | A **Going** label |
| Weather and transit cards | Stay | Fold away |

If you've turned weather or transit off in **Event Page Modules**, they stay off in both styles.

### Seeing Everything Again

Tap the arrow on the strip and the full details open. They stay open for that visit, and the page is folded again the next time you come back. Under the open strip, **Turn off in settings** takes you straight to the switch.

<Note>
  The Roll Call banner never folds. While Roll Call is waiting on your answer, the banner stays in full view above the strip. See [Roll Call](/features/roll-call).
</Note>

## Which Tab the Page Opens On

When you're going to an upcoming event that has an **On the Table** tab:

| You're Using | The Page Opens On |
| --- | --- |
| A phone, with the fold on | **On the Table** |
| A phone, with the fold off | The event's usual first tab |
| A computer | **On the Table** |

For everyone else, the page opens where it always has: **Overview** for most events, and **On the Table** for a Game Session.

A link to a specific tab still opens that tab. And if you switch to **Overview** yourself, refreshing the page keeps you there.

## The One-Time Invitation

On your phone, WR-Games asks you once whether you'd like the fold. It's called **Skip Straight to the Games**, and you'll see it in one of two places:

- **A sheet**, right after you RSVP **Going** to an upcoming event.
- **A card at the top of the page**, on an event you were already going to.

It starts on **Light**. Choose **Extended** if you'd rather, then answer:

| You Choose | What Happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Turn On Light** or **Turn On Extended** | The fold turns on in that style, and the page opens on **On the Table** |
| **Not Now** | Nothing changes |

WR-Games saves either answer to your account, so it won't ask you again on any of your devices. Close the sheet without answering and the invitation can come back on a later visit.

The invitation waits while Roll Call is asking you to respond.

---

## Turning the Fold Off

Tap **Turn off in settings** under the open strip, or go to **Settings → Privacy & Visibility → Event Page Modules**. Switch off **Fold event details when I'm going**.

The **Fold Style** row disappears, and WR-Games keeps your style for when you turn the fold back on. On a computer, events you're going to still open on **On the Table**.
