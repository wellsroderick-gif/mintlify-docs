Here's what's new on WR-Games — newest first.

<Update label="August 2026" tags={["Events","Notifications"]}>
  ## Rescheduling an event now re-notifies attendees

  When you move an event, we now notify attendees of the new time. A change of **24 hours or more** re-opens Roll Call for the new date, clearing earlier answers and asking everyone again — so your confirmed list reflects the time people are actually committing to. A change of **72 hours or more** re-sends the 24-hour reminders as well. Smaller adjustments stay quiet. [How Roll Call handles reschedules](https://help.wr-games.com/features/roll-call).

  ## Six more notifications now reach your bell

  Event reminders, waitlist confirmations, kudos requests, RSVP activity, organizer role updates, and leadership updates now appear in your notification bell alongside the email. If you don't watch your inbox closely, you'll still catch them. [Manage your notifications](https://help.wr-games.com/help/notifications).

  ## Clearer cancellation notices

  Everyone going or waitlisted gets the same clear notice when you cancel an event. Cancelling a series tells recipients the whole series has ended rather than naming a single date, and the **Not Going** link in a cancellation email now registers your response directly. [More on cancelling events](https://help.wr-games.com/features/creating-events).
</Update>

<Update label="July 2026" tags={["Events","Transit"]}>
  ## Transit options on event pages

  In-person event pages in the DC area now show transit options — the **Getting there** card gives you the nearest Metro station or bus stop, walking time, and the lines or routes served, plus live train and bus arrivals on the day of the event (with first and last departures so you know you can still get home). It's public, so anyone can see how to reach a venue. [See how it works](https://help.wr-games.com/features/transit).
</Update>

<Update label="June 2026" tags={["Integrations","Calendar","Platform"]}>
  ## Connect your Meetup group to WR-Games

  Already running your group on Meetup? You can now connect it to WR-Games and bring your events over — sync them in to sit side by side with your WR-Games events so members can RSVP without leaving, or simply link out to Meetup. A quick **Refresh** pulls the latest whenever you cancel or update something on Meetup. [See how the Meetup integration works](https://help.wr-games.com/groups/meetup-integration).

  ## Your BoardGameGeek collection, fully under your control

  Syncing your BGG collection is faster and far more flexible. Your games show up almost immediately while the details fill in behind the scenes, you choose which collections to pull when you first connect, and you can reset or wipe your synced collection whenever you want. [Learn how BGG sync and your Collection work](https://help.wr-games.com/features/bgg-collection).

  ## Add custom entries to your group calendar

  Mark up your group's calendar with custom entries — birthdays, reminders, social nights, whatever your group needs. They save automatically, show up for everyone in the group, and flow into any subscribed calendar feed. [See group calendar custom entries](https://help.wr-games.com/groups/group-calendar-custom-entries).

  ## A new support center at help.wr-games.com

  We launched a support center — clear, searchable guides for groups, events, RSVPs, integrations, and more, so you can get an answer fast instead of guessing. This changelog lives here too. [Visit the support center](https://help.wr-games.com).
</Update>
