About four hours before an event starts, WR-Games asks everyone who's Going to confirm they're still coming. Hosts get a headcount from that afternoon instead of one from days ago.

Roll Call runs on every published event, whether it belongs to a group or someone hosts it themselves. There's nothing to turn on. People on the waitlist are asked too, whether they're still interested.

## How You're Asked

| Channel | Who Gets It |
| --- | --- |
| In-app notification | Everyone who's asked |
| Email | Everyone who's asked |
| Text message | Once you've [turned on text messages](#turning-on-text-messages) |

<Note>
  During the beta, Roll Call texts are turned on for every group and for events members host on their own. Text messages are normally a paid feature, so this may change. You never need a paid plan of your own to get them.
</Note>

## When Roll Call Opens

- **About 4 hours before the start,** automatically.
- **Earlier, if the host opens it.** Hosts can open Roll Call by hand up to 8 hours before the start.
- **Not at all** for an event published less than 6 hours before it starts.

Cancelling an event clears its Roll Call.

## For Attendees: How to Confirm Your Attendance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Prompt">
    Open the notification, the email or the link in the text message. Each one takes you to the event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Still Attending">
    The **Roll Call** banner on the event page asks you to confirm you're still coming. Select **Still attending**, and a green check appears beside your name on the attendee list. In the email, **I'm Still Coming** does the same thing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Can't Make It? Say So">
    Select **Can't make it** instead. The host's headcount stays accurate, and your spot goes to someone on the waitlist.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Replying to the text message doesn't answer Roll Call. Use the link in it.

If you're on the waitlist, the banner asks whether you're still interested instead, with **Still interested** and **Not interested**.

## For Organizers: Reading the Confirmed List

On the event's Attendees tab, everyone who confirmed has a green check beside their name. Anyone without one hasn't answered yet. They're still Going, so follow up or plan conservatively. The list updates as answers come in, right up until the event starts.

Use the confirmed list to plan tables in [On the Table](/features/on-the-table), arrange seating and decide how many copies of a game you'll need in play at once.

For a read on who's likely to come before Roll Call even opens, see [Predictive Attendance](/features/predictive-attendance).

### When You Change Someone's Guests

While Roll Call is open, changing how many guests a Going attendee is bringing gives them the green check, the same as adding someone to Going. It counts as you having checked with them. This applies whether you use **Edit Guests** on the Attendees tab or add a guest of theirs from On the Table.

The attendee isn't notified. Naming a guest they already had room for doesn't change their check.

## What Each Action Does to Your Check

Roll Call is open from the moment it starts until the event begins. While it's open, a check beside a name means someone answered. Green means confirmed as Going. Orange means still interested, on the waitlist.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="You Answer">
    - **Still attending**: green check
    - **Can't make it**: you move to Not Going
    - **Still interested**, on the waitlist: orange check
    - **Not interested**, on the waitlist: you move to Not Going
    - You change your own RSVP into an open spot: green check
    - You change your own RSVP onto the waitlist: orange check
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="An Organizer Acts">
    - Moves or adds you to Going: green check. The organizer has confirmed for you.
    - Moves or adds you to the waitlist: orange check
    - Adds you as a Co-Host, or makes you the host: green check
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="You're Promoted">
    - You'd already answered **Still interested**: green check when you move up to Going
    - You hadn't answered: no check. You're asked to confirm.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
  Outside open Roll Call, changing an RSVP or moving someone doesn't add a check. Hosts listed on the event are confirmed automatically when Roll Call opens.
</Info>

## When an Event Is Rescheduled

If a host moves an event by **24 hours or more**, earlier or later, Roll Call starts over:

- **Every earlier confirmation is cleared,** and everyone is asked again for the new date.
- **Hosts listed on the event are confirmed automatically** for the new date.
- **A fresh Roll Call opens** about 4 hours before the new start.

Moves of **less than 24 hours** change nothing. Moving a start from 7:00 to 7:30 keeps every confirmation and sends no new prompt.

<Note>
  If the move puts the new start less than 4 hours away, the fresh prompt goes out on the next scheduled pass rather than the moment the host saves. Give it a few minutes.
</Note>

## Roll Call and Your Trust Score

<Warning>
  Confirming through Roll Call and then not coming counts as a **no-show** and hurts your Trust Score. Only confirm if you plan to be there. If something comes up after you confirm, update your RSVP or message the host as soon as you can.
</Warning>

See [Trust and Reputation](/concepts/trust-and-reputation) for how attendance affects your score.

## Turning On Text Messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify Your Phone">
    In **Settings**, open the **Account** tab and verify your phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title={"Open Privacy & Visibility"}>
    Go to the **Privacy & Visibility** tab and find **SMS notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On SMS Notifications and Roll Call">
    Turn on **SMS Notifications**, then turn on **Roll Call**. **SMS Notifications** stays greyed out until your phone is verified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Roll Call** switch also covers the text you get when you come off the waitlist. There's no separate switch for that.

Reply **STOP** to any WR-Games text to stop all texts, or turn the switches off in the same place.
