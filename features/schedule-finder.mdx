Schedule Finder is a poll for picking a time. You offer a few times, share one link, and everyone marks which ones work for them. Once a time is picked, you can turn it into an event in a couple of taps.

Anyone can start a poll or answer one, with or without a WR-Games account.

<Info>
  **Start a poll in a group, or on its own.**

  - **In a group:** on the group's **Schedule Finder** tab, select **Find a Time**. Only that group's members can answer.
  - **On its own:** open **Schedule Finder** on the **Apps** page, or select **Find a Time** on the **Events** page. Anyone with the link can answer, with or without an account. If you're signed in, you can pick one of your groups here instead.
</Info>

---

## Where to Find It

| Where | What It Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Find a Time** on the **Events** page | A new poll (shown when you're signed in) |
| **Schedule Finder** on the **Apps** page | A new poll, signed in or not |
| The **Polls** tab in **My Events** | Every poll you started or answered, open and closed |
| A group's **Schedule Finder** tab | That group's polls |

---

## Two Kinds of Poll

When you start a poll signed in, **Who Is It For** asks where it lives:

- **Anyone With the Link**: anyone who has the link can answer, with or without an account. The poll doesn't belong to a group.
- **One of Your Groups**: only that group's members can answer. Only groups where you're allowed to start polls are listed. Then **Who Can Vote** sets who's in: **Everyone in the Group** or **Specific Members**.

A poll stays where you start it. It can't be moved into a group later.

If you're not signed in, your poll is always **Anyone With the Link**.

## Starting a Poll

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Find a Time">
    From the **Events** page, the **Apps** page or a group's **Schedule Finder** tab. Not signed in? Choose **Continue Without an Account**, or **Sign In** first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name It">
    Give the poll a name. A description is optional. Without an account, you also enter **Your Name** so people know who's asking.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick Times">
    Choose a **Length** (**30 Min**, **1 Hr**, **2 Hr** or **Custom**), then select a start time to add each block. Times can start on the half hour, and they can overlap. A poll holds up to 40 times.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a Place, Games and a Due Date">
    All three are optional. A place can be a **Venue**, an **Address** or **Online**. **Answers Due** sets when answering stops.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Your Options">
    Set the switches below, then create the poll. You get a link to share right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You count as **Yes** on every time you offer, including any you add later.

Each poll uses one time zone, shown on the form as **Time Zone**. It starts as yours, and you can change it before you create the poll. Everyone sees the times in the poll's zone, with its short name, like EST or EDT.

### Options When You Make a Poll

| Switch | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Show Answers to Everyone** | On: everyone sees the best times and who answered. Off: only you see who answered, and everyone else sees just their own answer. |
| **Pick the Best Time When Answers Close** | When the due date arrives, the time with the most Yes answers is picked for you. Needs a due date. |
| **Require Sign-In to Answer** | Only people with an account can answer, so everyone who answers can be notified. Only on **Anyone With the Link** polls. |
| **Auto RSVP Respondents** | If you make an event from the poll, people who said Yes to the picked time are RSVP'd for you. See [Auto RSVP](#auto-rsvp). |

These four are set when you make the poll. Edit Poll doesn't show them.

## Starting a Poll Without an Account

You get two links when the poll is ready:

- **Share Link**: send this to everyone you want to answer.
- **Manage Link**: keep this one to yourself. It's the only way back to edit the poll, close it or pick a time.

<Warning>
  Without an account, the poll and both links stop working after 14 days. Select **Sign In to Save This Poll** to keep it on your account, in My Events, with no time limit.
</Warning>

A poll started without an account doesn't send you any notifications. Open your manage link to see the answers. You also need to be signed in to invite people by name or email.

## Sharing and Inviting

Every poll has a share link. **Copy Link** or **Share** from the poll's page.

On an **Anyone With the Link** poll, a signed-in organizer can also invite people:

- **Invite People You Play With**: your friends, and members of groups where you can invite people. They get a notification and an email.
- **Invite by Email**: up to 25 addresses per poll, and 50 a day.

An invite doesn't change who can answer. The share link always works.

On a group poll with **Specific Members**, the people you pick are the only ones who can see and answer it. You can add more people while it's open.

## Answering a Poll

Open the link and you'll see **When Can You Play?** Mark each time **Yes**, **Maybe** or **No**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Signed In">
    Your answer is saved to your account. You can change it any time while the poll is open with **Edit Your Answer**, and you'll be notified when a time is picked.

    Select **Also Text Me** to get a text as well. You need a verified phone number for that, and **Verify a Number** sets one up.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Without an Account">
    Enter **Your Name** (a first name or nickname is fine) and answer.

    Your answer is final once you leave the page. While you're still on it, **Sign In to Save Your Answer** makes it yours, so you can change it later and get notified. Otherwise, check the link again to see the picked time.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A time that has already started is dimmed and marked **Past**. You can't mark it, and any answer you gave on it before stays.

If the organizer turned on **Require Sign-In to Answer**, you'll see **Sign In to Answer This Poll** instead of the times.

### Seeing the Answers

**Best Times So Far** ranks the times by Yes answers, then Maybe. **Who Answered** lists everyone, with members and guests shown apart.

If the organizer turned **Show Answers to Everyone** off, you see a confirmation and your own answer only.

## Managing Your Poll

Open **Manage Poll** from the poll's page, or your manage link if you started it without an account. From there:

| Action | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Change the name, description, place, games, due date and times while the poll is open |
| **Share** / **Invite** | Send the link, or invite people (signed in only) |
| **Close Poll** | Stop answers (see below) |
| **Duplicate** | Start a new poll with the same details |
| **Delete Poll** | Remove the poll |

When you remove a time, its answers go with it. The page asks you to confirm first. When you save your changes, **Notify Everyone?** asks whether to tell the people who've answered. Choose **Save Without Notifying** to skip it.

In **Who Answered**, you can remove a guest's answer with **Remove Answer**. It comes off every time, and they can answer again from the share link.

## Closing a Poll and Picking a Time

Select **Close Poll** and choose how:

- **Pick the Best Time for Me**: picks the time with the most Yes answers among the times still ahead (Maybe answers break a tie, then the earliest time), closes the poll and tells everyone who answered.
- **I'll Choose**: closes the poll so you can pick a time yourself.

**Pick the Best Time for Me** is off when nobody else has answered yet, or when every time has passed. The dialog says which.

You can also pick any time directly: tap it, then select **Pick This Time**. That closes the poll and picks the time in one step.

### Due Dates and Automatic Picking

**Answers Due** stops new answers. It can be no later than the start of the poll's last time. If you remove the latest time while editing, you may be asked to move the due date earlier.

With **Pick the Best Time When Answers Close** on, the best time still ahead is picked when answers close. A time that has passed is never picked, and there are two cases where nothing is picked:

| If… | What Happens |
| --- | --- |
| Every time has passed | The poll closes and you're told to duplicate it with new times (**Every Time Has Passed**, then **Duplicate Poll**) |
| Nobody else answered | The poll closes, and you get an "Answers are in" notice to pick a time yourself |

With automatic picking off, the poll closes on its due date and you get the same "Answers are in" notice.

### When a Time Is Picked

Everyone who answered while signed in gets a notification and an email. Anyone who selected **Also Text Me** gets a text too. Guests open the link again to see the picked time.

## Schedule the Game Night

Once a time is picked, select **Schedule It** to make the event. The event wizard opens with the title, time and place already filled in, and you can change anything before you publish.

On an **Anyone With the Link** poll, you first choose **Who Can See the Event**: **Public**, **Unlisted** or **Invite-only**. A group poll's event follows the group's settings.

What happens to the people who answered depends on Auto RSVP.

### Auto RSVP

When you make a poll, **Auto RSVP Respondents** decides whether the people who say Yes are RSVP'd for you. It starts off.

**With it on:**

- Before anyone answers, the poll tells them: **Auto RSVP is turned on.** If an event is created and they said Yes to the time you pick, they're RSVP'd automatically, as long as there's room.
- When you make the event, people who said Yes to the picked time go **Going** in the order they answered, until the event is full. After that, they go on the waitlist.
- People who said **Maybe** go on the waitlist when the event has a capacity.
- Only people who answered while signed in are RSVP'd. Guests are told that without an account, they can't be.
- Until you make the event, people who said Yes to the picked time see **No Event Yet** on the poll page.

**With it off**, **Schedule It** gives you three choices:

| Choice | What Happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Invite People** | Choose who to invite. People who said Yes to the picked time come first. |
| **Tell Everyone Who Said Yes** | Everyone signed in who said Yes is told the event is ready, and they RSVP themselves. |
| **Neither** | The event is made, and you share its link yourself. |

**Invite People** needs you to be signed in. Either way, once the event exists it works like any other event: RSVPs, the waitlist and [Roll Call](/features/roll-call) all follow the usual rules.

## Privacy and Safety

- Poll pages are kept out of search engines.
- Something wrong with a poll? Select **Report** on its page (you need to be signed in).
- Names typed by people without an account are kept with the poll. The [Privacy Policy](https://wr-games.com/privacy) has the details.
