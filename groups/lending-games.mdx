Members of a group can lend each other games and keep a record of who has what. It runs on the honor system: WR-Games tracks the loan, and the two of you handle the handover.

You'll find it on the group's **Collection** tab, under **Loans**.

## Two Ways a Loan Starts

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Lend a Game">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Lend a Game">
        Type the game's name. Search the directory if you'd like its cover on the loan.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the Borrower">
        Pick the member who's taking it home.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the Details">
        A **Due Date** and **Shared Details** are both optional.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Create Loan">
        The loan is active right away.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    If you already have that game out to someone in the group, WR-Games checks that you mean to lend another copy.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Request to Borrow">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Request to Borrow">
        Type the game's name, or search the directory.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the Lender">
        Pick the member you're asking.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Send Request">
        The lender can approve or decline.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    One open request per person per game. Once it's answered, you can ask again.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Answering a Request

If someone asks to borrow from you, the request shows **Approve** and **Decline**. Approving makes the loan active.

Declining gives you two choices:

| Choice | What Happens |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Decline With a Note** | The borrower is notified and can read your note. Only the two of you can read it. |
| **Decline Quietly** | No notification. The request drops off the borrower's list. |

## Who Can See What

| Part of a Loan | Who Sees It |
| :-- | :-- |
| The loan itself and its **Shared Details** | Everyone in the group |
| Your private note | Only you. Not the other person, and not the group's organizers. |
| Comments | Only the lender and the borrower |

The lender and the borrower can open **Details** on a loan to add a private note or leave a comment. You can delete your own comment, but not edit it.

Once a loan exists, the lender is the one who changes its shared details and due date.

## Due Dates and Reminders

A due date is optional. When there is one:

- The borrower gets a reminder as the date approaches, and a notice once it has passed.
- The lender is told if the game is still out after that.
- An **Overdue** marker shows on the loan for the two of you.

If the lender changes the due date, the reminders follow the new date.

<Note>
  Loan notifications arrive in WR-Games only. There's no loan email.
</Note>

## Returning a Game

Either of you can select **Mark Returned**. The loan moves to **History**, where its details can still be read but no longer changed.

If you leave the group, your open loans there are closed, and you can no longer see that group's loan history.
