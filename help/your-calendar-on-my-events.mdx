**My Events** has a **Calendar** view that puts everything on one month grid: events you're hosting, going to, waitlisted for or saved, past and cancelled events, and your groups' calendar entries. Switch to it from **List** at the top of the page.

## Choosing What Shows

The key above the grid lists each kind of entry, each in its own color:

**Hosting**, **Going**, **Waitlisted**, **Saved**, **Past**, **Cancelled** and **Group Entries**

Click a name to hide it. Click it again to bring it back. A hidden name stays in the key, struck through, so you can always find it.

<Tip>
  This only changes what you see on the grid. It doesn't change what goes to your calendar app.
</Tip>

## Subscribe in Your Calendar App

Your calendar feed is a private link that keeps your calendar app in step with WR-Games. Select the gear icon beside the grid to open **Calendar Settings**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn It On">
    Select **Enable Calendar Feed**. Nothing is created until you do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Link">
    Select the copy button beside **Feed Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add It to Your Calendar App">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Google Calendar">
        Next to **Other calendars**, choose **+**, then **From URL**, and paste the link.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Apple Calendar">
        Choose **File → New Calendar Subscription** and paste the link.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Outlook">
        Choose **Add calendar**, then **Subscribe from web**, and paste the link.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Google Calendar names a subscribed calendar after the link itself. You can rename it in Google Calendar's settings for that calendar.
</Note>

Your calendar app checks for changes on its own schedule, so an update on WR-Games shows up there the next time it checks.

<Warning>
  Anyone with your feed link can see your events. Keep it to yourself.
</Warning>

### What's in the Feed

| Included | Not Included |
| :-- | :-- |
| Events you're hosting or going to, past ones too | Saved events |
| Cancelled events, shown as cancelled | Drafts |
| Waitlisted events, if you turn them on | |
| Your groups' calendar entries, group by group | |

**Include Waitlisted Events** starts off. Turn it on and those events appear as tentative.

Under **Group Calendar Entries**, each of your groups has its own switch for its birthdays, reminders and other dates. Turning a group off only removes those entries. Events you're attending from that group always stay.

### Changing or Stopping the Feed

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Regenerate Link" icon="refresh-cw">
    Your old link stops working straight away. Subscribe again with the new one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Turn Off Feed" icon="power">
    Your link stops working straight away. You can turn the feed back on any time.
  </Card>
</Columns>
