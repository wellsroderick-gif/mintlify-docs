Not every community wants to be open to the world, and not every group should be hard to find. WR-Games gives you three visibility levels so you can match your group's privacy to its purpose: whether that's a wide-open public club, a members-only community that still appears in search, or a completely hidden group that only exists for those in the know. Pair any visibility level with invite links and join approval to get exactly the access model you need.

## Visibility Levels Compared

| Visibility | In directory? | Who can see content? | How to join? |
|-----------|:-------------:|:--------------------:|:------------:|
| **Public** | Yes | Anyone | Open join or request |
| **Private** | Yes | Members only | Request to join |
| **Hidden** | No | Members only | Invite link only (Pro) |

### Public

Public groups appear in the WR-Games group directory and in search results. Anyone browsing the platform can see your group name, description, tags, and upcoming events. Members can join instantly if you have open join enabled, or submit a request if you require approval. The choice is yours.

### Private

Private groups still show up in the directory and search, so people can discover that your group exists. However, content (events, forum threads, chat, and member lists) is only visible to members. Prospective members must submit a join request, which an organizer reviews before granting access.

### Hidden

Hidden groups are completely invisible to non-members. They don't appear in the directory, in search results, or anywhere else on the platform. The only way to join is through an invitation from the group. Hidden groups are ideal for closed playtesting circles, private game clubs, or invite-only competitive leagues.

<Note>
  Hidden groups require a **Pro plan**. Public and Private visibility are available on all plans, including Free.
</Note>

<Warning>
  If you cancel your paid plan, hidden groups you organize are deleted, along with their events, 30 days after your paid period ends. Moving to a lower paid plan doesn't do this. See [What Happens When You Cancel](/billing/what-happens-when-you-cancel) for the timeline and how to keep a group.
</Warning>

## Inviting People

An invitation brings someone straight into your group. They join the moment they accept, with no approval wait, even if your group normally reviews requests. That works for public and private groups as well as hidden ones.

You'll find everything under the gear icon on your group's page, in **Privacy & Access**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Specific People">
    On the **Invite People** card, select **Choose People**. Pick people by name or type an email address, then send. Each person gets an invitation addressed to them.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="A Link With a Limit">
    On the same card, under **Invite Link**, enter a number in **Max uses** or leave it blank for an unlimited link. Select **Create Link** and it's copied for you to share. Anyone with the link can join until it's used up.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Hidden Group Link">
    Hidden groups also have a standing **Invite Link** card. Select **Generate Invite Link**, then copy the link. **Reset Link** replaces it, and the old link stops working right away.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Who Can Invite

Organizers and Co-Organizers can invite by default. Other members can't unless you turn on **Invite members** for their role. See [Roles & Permissions](/features/roles-and-permissions).

### Keeping Track of Invitations

The **Invitations** list on the **Invite People** card shows every invitation and link, who sent it, and where it stands.

| Status | What It Means |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Pending** | Sent, not yet accepted |
| **Accepted** | The person joined |
| **Fully Used** | A link that has reached its limit |
| **Revoked** | You cancelled it |

A link also lists who joined with it under **Joined By**. To cancel an invitation or a link that's travelled too far, select the trash can beside it. Members who already joined stay in the group.

<Note>
  A group can only have so many invitations waiting at once. If you're getting close, the card tells you how many more you can send. Room frees up when someone accepts or you revoke one. Invite links don't count toward the limit.
</Note>

### Accepting an Invitation

Open the invitation and select **Accept Invitation**. You're in the group right away. If the group asks join questions, you'll answer them on the way in.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I'm Not Signed In">
    Select **Sign In to Accept**, then sign in or create an account. You come back to the invitation afterward. Select **Accept Invitation** once to join.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I Left the Invitation Without Accepting">
    The group's page goes back to its normal way of joining, which may be a request the group reviews. Open your invitation link again to use the invitation instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It Says the Link Is No Longer Valid">
    The invitation was revoked or the link has been used up. Ask the organizer for a new one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Join Approval for Public Groups

Even if your group is Public, you can require organizer approval before someone officially becomes a member. This is useful when you want community discoverability but still want to vet new members.

To enable approval on a public group, go to **Settings → Join Settings** and switch the join mode to **Request Required**. Incoming requests will appear in **Group → Members → Pending Requests**, where you can approve or decline each one. On Standard and Pro plans, you'll also see each applicant's Trust Score to help you decide.

You can also set up a **group questionnaire**: a short set of questions applicants answer when requesting to join. Responses appear alongside the join request, giving you useful context before you approve.

## Event Visibility by Group Type

Your group's privacy setting also affects how your events appear across the platform:

- **Public group events** are listed publicly on WR-Games and may appear in search results and the events directory. Anyone can view event details.
- **Private and Hidden group events** are only visible to current members. Non-members cannot see that the event exists, even if they have the direct event URL.
