You don't have to run your venue's page alone. Invite your managers and staff to the team, give each of them a role, and they can help from Manage.

Everything here happens on the **Team** tab in Manage. If you haven't been in Manage before, start with [Run Your Venue](/features/running-your-venue).

<Note>
  Only the venue's owner manages the team. Team members can see who else is on it, but they can't invite, remove or change anyone.
</Note>

## Inviting People to Your Team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team Tab">
    In Manage, open **Team** and select **Invite to Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Role">
    Choose the role they'll have. **Manager** is picked for you to start. See [The Roles](#the-roles) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose People">
    Select **Choose People** and pick who to invite. Everyone you invite needs a WR-Games account. You can't invite by email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An invite shows on your team list as **waiting to accept**. The person accepts or declines it on your venue's page. Once they accept, **Manage Venue** shows up for them, and your venue appears under **Venues You Run** in their My Venues.

Changed your mind before they accept? Open the menu next to their name and choose **Revoke Invite**.

## The Roles

Every venue starts with five roles.

| Role | What They Can Do |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Co-Owner** | Everything a role can do. This role can't be changed. |
| **Manager** | Edit the profile, hours, images and amenities, set a temporary closure, and run the calendar feed |
| **Event Planner** | Manage the venue's events |
| **Moderator** | Handle reports and flags sent to the venue |
| **Team Member** | Open Manage and see the team, but change nothing |

Some permissions are for features that aren't open yet, like reservations, tables and reviews. They do nothing until those features arrive.

### Changing What a Role Can Do

In the **Roles** card on the Team tab, select **Edit** next to a role and tick the permissions you want it to have. The five starting roles keep their names, but you can change what any of them can do except Co-Owner.

### Making Your Own Role

Select **New Role**, give it a name and tick its permissions. You can delete a role you made, but only once nobody holds it. Move everyone to another role first.

### What No Role Can Do

Some things stay with the owner, whatever role someone holds:

- Managing the team
- Transferring ownership
- Closing the venue for good

## Changing Someone's Role or Removing Them

Open the menu next to their name on the Team tab.

- **Change Role:** pick their new role. It takes effect right away.
- **Remove From Team:** they lose access to Manage right away. Anything they already changed stays.

## What Team Members See

- **Manage Venue** on the venue's page, and the venue under **Venues You Run** in My Venues
- Only the Manage tabs their role allows. Tabs they can't use are hidden.
- A read-only list of the team on the Team tab

Your team isn't shown on the public venue page.

## Handing Your Venue to Someone Else

If someone else is taking over, transfer ownership to them. They need to be on your team first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In Manage, open **Settings**. Under **Transfer Ownership**, select **Transfer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the New Owner">
    Pick them from **New Owner**. Only people on your team appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select **Transfer Ownership**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The transfer happens right away, and the new owner doesn't have to accept it. You stay on the team as **Co-Owner**, but the new owner runs the team from then on, and they can remove you.
</Warning>

The rest of the team stays, with the same roles.

### Leaving the Team After a Transfer

Once you've handed the venue over, you can leave whenever you like. On the Team tab, select **Leave Team**. You lose access to Manage right away, and your past edits stay.

Other team members can't leave on their own. If you're on a team and want off it, ask the venue's owner to remove you.

## Requesting Your Venue's Removal

If your venue should come off WR-Games altogether, open **Settings** in Manage and select **Request Removal**. Tell us why, then select **Send Request**.

This opens a support request. Staff decide, work with the hosts of any events linked to your venue, and reply in your inbox.

If you submitted a venue but don't own it, you can request its removal too. Look for **Request Removal** in the venue page's **⋯** menu.
