Sometimes an Organizer stays on WR-Games but stops looking after one of their groups. When that happens, members can ask to take over. Customer Service reviews every request and decides who gets offered the role.

<Note>
  If your Organizer has stopped signing in to WR-Games altogether, this page doesn't apply. The [Group Activity and Retention Policy](/groups/group-activity-policy) covers that case.
</Note>

## When You Can Ask

Requests open once your Organizer has gone about 90 days without visiting the group. After that, who can ask depends on how long requests have been open:

| Time since requests opened | Who can ask |
| --- | --- |
| First 7 days | Co-Organizers |
| Day 7 to day 37 | Any member |

This works the same whatever plan your Organizer is on, including paid plans and the Founder plan.

The **Leadership & transfer** card appears about a month before requests open, so you can see the dates coming.

## Sending a Request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Group's Settings">
    From your group's page, open the group's settings. If you're a member, this takes you to **My Preferences**. If you're a Co-Organizer, it opens the group's management page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Leadership & Transfer">
    Look for the **Leadership & transfer** card. It shows the dates requests are open and who can ask right now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say Why You Should Lead">
    Under **Why should you lead this group?**, write a few sentences about how you'd run the group. Then select **Submit leadership request**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The card calls your request a petition. Once it's sent, the card shows your place in line, for example "#1 in queue."

A few limits apply:

- You can have one request open at a time.
- A group can have up to three requests waiting. If the line is full, try again later.
- Nobody can ask while another Organizer hand-off is already under way in the group.

Your Organizer gets an email and a notification when someone sends a request, with a reminder to visit the group.

## After You Ask

**If your request is approved,** you'll get an email and a notification. Open the group's page and accept the Organizer role from the offer at the top. Taking on a group counts toward your plan's group limit, so if you're already at your limit, you'll need to upgrade first. See [Plans & Billing](/billing/plans) for the limits.

**If it isn't approved,** you'll get an email and a notification. You can ask again while requests are still open.

## If Your Organizer Comes Back

<Info>
  If your Organizer visits the group again, no new requests can be sent. Requests that were already sent are still reviewed.
</Info>

## If Nobody Takes the Role

<Warning>
  If the role is offered and nobody accepts it in time, the group falls under the [Group Activity and Retention Policy](/groups/group-activity-policy) and is deleted.
</Warning>
