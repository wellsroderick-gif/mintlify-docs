Every event at your venue carries a label that tells members who's running it. The **Events** tab in Manage is where you control those labels, and where you list the groups and people you sponsor so their events stand out.

If you haven't been in Manage before, start with [Run Your Venue](/features/running-your-venue).

## The Three Labels

| Label | What It Means |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Official** | Your venue runs the event |
| **Partner** | A group or person you sponsor runs it |
| **Community-Led** | Anyone else hosting at your venue |

Members see the label on event cards across your venue page. Labels only mark a card. They never change the order, so events always list by date.

## Who Can Use the Events Tab

The owner, and anyone on the team whose role includes **Manage Events**. The **Event Planner** role has it to start. [Run Your Venue With a Team](/features/run-your-venue-with-a-team) covers roles.

## Adding a Partner

Partners are the groups and people you sponsor. Once someone is a partner, their upcoming events at your venue show the **Partner** label.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Partners">
    In Manage, open **Events**. In the **Partners** card, select **Add Partner**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Group or a Person">
    On **Group**, search public groups by name and select the one you want. On **Person**, select **Choose a Person** and pick them. They need a WR-Games account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each partner shows how many upcoming events they have at your venue.

### Removing a Partner

Open the menu next to their name and choose **Remove Partner**. Their upcoming events at your venue go back to **Community-Led**.

## Changing an Event's Label

The **Event Labels** card lists the upcoming events at your venue, with each one's date, time and host.

To change a label, pick **Official**, **Partner** or **Community-Led** next to the event. The new label stays until the event's host changes or your partner list does.

<Note>
  Creating events as your venue isn't open yet. The **Official Events** row at the top of the tab shows **Create Event** greyed out until it is. In the meantime, you can mark an event **Official** from the Event Labels card.
</Note>
