If you run a game store, café, library or anywhere else people come to play, there's a good chance WR-Games already has a page for it. Players add venues to the directory so others can find a place to play.

Claiming that page makes it yours. It shows as **Official**, and you can keep its hours, details and photos up to date yourself. If your venue isn't listed yet, you can add it and own it from the start.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="It's Already Listed" icon="store">
    Open its page and select **Claim This Venue**. See [Claiming a Listed Venue](#claiming-a-listed-venue) below.
  </Card>

  <Card title="It Isn't Listed Yet" icon="plus">
    Go to **Submit a Venue** and choose **I Own This Venue**. See [Adding Your Own Venue](#adding-your-own-venue) below.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Who Can Claim

Any signed-in member can claim a venue that doesn't have an owner yet. You don't need to be the owner on paper. Managers and staff can claim too.

A venue that already has an owner doesn't show **Claim This Venue**. If that owner isn't you, see [If Someone Else Already Runs It](#if-someone-else-already-runs-it).

## Claiming a Listed Venue

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Venue's Page">
    Find your venue in the Venues directory and select **Claim This Venue**.

    You can also start from **Submit a Venue**. Choose **Claim a Listed Venue** and you're taken to the directory to find it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Us How You're Connected">
    Pick **Owner**, **Manager** or **Staff**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass One Check">
    Choose one of the checks on the screen. Each one sends a code to contact details already on the listing, or asks you to place a code on the venue's website. The table below covers each one.

    No access to any of them? Choose **Upload Documents** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Your Authority">
    Tick **I have authority to manage this venue.** You can't continue without it, but on its own it never approves a claim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    If you passed a check, your claim is approved on the spot. If you're sending documents, add them now and submit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The Checks

You only see the checks this venue's listing supports. If the listing has no email, for example, there's no email check.

| Check | How It Works | Offered When |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Email a Code** | We email a 6-digit code to the venue's listed email. It works for 10 minutes. | The listed email matches the venue's website |
| **Text or Call** | Choose **Text Me** or **Call Me**, and we send a 6-digit code to the venue's listed phone. It works for 10 minutes. | The listing has a phone number |
| **Add a Code to Your Website** | Select **Get My Code**, then add it to your site as a **Homepage Tag** or a **DNS Record**. Select **Check Now** once it's in place. | The listing has a website |

<Tip>
  Code didn't arrive? Select **Send a New Code**. A venue's phone can only receive so many codes in a day. If it hits that limit, try again tomorrow or use a different check.
</Tip>

### Claiming With Documents

If none of the checks work for you, upload proof instead: a business license, utility bill or lease that shows the venue's name and address.

- **File types:** PDF, JPG or PNG
- **Size:** up to 10 MB each
- **How many:** up to 5 files per claim

Only you and WR-Games staff can see your documents.

## What Happens Next

| How You Claimed | What You See | What Changes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Passed a check | **Claim Approved** | You run the venue right away. Its page shows as Official and Manage is open. Choose **Open Manage** to start. |
| Sent documents | **Claim Submitted** | Staff review your documents. The venue page stays as it is until they do. Choose **View My Venues** to follow your claim. |

<Note>
  Staff confirm every approved claim afterwards. You don't need to wait for them to start running your venue.
</Note>

## Adding Your Own Venue

If your venue isn't on WR-Games yet, add it yourself and skip the claim.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a Submission">
    Go to **Submit a Venue** and choose **I Own This Venue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill In Your Venue's Details">
    Same details as any venue submission: name, location, hours, contact details and what the venue offers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm You Run It">
    On the review step, tick **I have authority to manage this venue.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Your venue goes live as Official, with you as its owner. You land on **Claim Approved**, with **Open Manage** ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### If It Turns Out to Be Listed Already

If the venue you're adding is already in the directory, you'll see **Already in Our Directory** instead of a new page.

- **No owner yet:** choose **Claim This Venue** and you go straight into the claim steps above.
- **Already has an owner, or still waiting for review:** there's nothing to claim. See [If Someone Else Already Runs It](#if-someone-else-already-runs-it).

## Following Your Claim in My Venues

Open **My Venues** from your account menu. Only you can see this page. It has up to three sections, and a section only shows when it has something in it.

| Section | What's in It |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Venues You Run** | Venues you own or are on the team for. **Manage** opens each one. |
| **Claims** | Claims you've started that aren't approved yet. Once a claim is approved, the venue moves up to Venues You Run. |
| **Submissions** | Venues you've added to the directory. [Editing and Resubmitting a Venue Submission](/features/editing-and-resubmitting-a-venue-submission) covers these. |

### Claim Statuses

| Status | What It Means | What You Can Do |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Verification in Progress** | You started a claim but haven't passed a check or sent documents yet. | **Continue Claim** |
| **In Review** | Staff are looking at your documents. | **View Claim** |
| **More Info Needed** | Staff need more proof before they can decide. | **Add Documents** |
| **Not Approved** | Staff turned the claim down. Your claim page shows their reason. | **View Claim** |

**View Claim** opens your claim page: the venue, its status, how you're connected, when you submitted it and the documents you've sent.

## When Staff Ask for More Information

Your claim shows **More Info Needed**, and your claim page shows **What Staff Need** with their message. Select **Add Documents** from My Venues or from the claim page and add what they asked for. Your claim stays open while you do.

The 5-file limit covers the whole claim, including anything you sent the first time. If you've hit it and staff still need something, [contact us](/help/contact).

## If Someone Else Already Runs It

If your venue already has an owner and it isn't you, there's no Claim button. [Contact us](/help/contact) and tell us which venue it is. Staff can look into it.
