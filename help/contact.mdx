There are three ways to get in touch. Which one you want depends on whether you have an account and what kind of problem you're having.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Support request" icon="inbox">
    For any question or account issue. Opens a case in your inbox, and replies come back to the same thread.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Bug reporter" icon="bug">
    For something visibly broken. Also opens a case, and records the page you were on when you hit the problem.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Contact form" icon="mail">
    For when you don't have an account. Replies come by email, so nothing appears on WR-Games.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
  Before reaching out, check [Common Issues](/help/common-issues) — sign-in trouble, BGG sync, RSVPs, and notifications are all covered there with step-by-step fixes.
</Tip>

## How to send one

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Support request">
    Use this for anything at all — a question, a billing problem, something about your account or a group you're in. You need to be signed in.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open your inbox">
        Go to **Messages** and select the **Inbox** tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start a request">
        Click **New support request** in the toolbar above your message list.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Describe it">
        Give it a short title and fill in the details. There's no category to pick — tell us what's going on in your own words.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **What to include.** What you were trying to do, what happened instead, and the name or link of the group or event if it's about one. You don't need to give us your name or email — the case is already tied to your account.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bug reporter">
    Use this when something on the site is broken: a button that does nothing, a page that won't load, a number that looks wrong. You need to be signed in.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Go to the page with the problem">
        The reporter records where you were, so send it from the screen where things went wrong.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the reporter">
        Click the floating bug button. It's available on any page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick a category and describe it">
        Choosing what kind of thing broke helps the report reach the right place faster.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **What to include.** What you expected to happen and what happened instead. You don't need to describe where you were or give us your details — both are captured for you.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Contact form">
    Use this if you don't have an account, or can't get into the one you have. It's open to anyone.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Go to the form">
        Open [wr-games.com/contact](https://wr-games.com/contact).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Fill it in">
        Tell us what you need and how to reach you.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **What to include.** Your account email address matters most here — without it we have no way to find you. Add your display name, the group or event in question, and a description of the problem.

    <Warning>
      A message sent this way won't appear in a WR-Games inbox and won't have a case number. The reply comes to your email instead.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Following your case

Support requests and bug reports both open a case in your inbox, with a case number (something like `CASE-10842`) at the top of the thread. That number is worth quoting if you need to refer back to it.

Each case shows a status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | We've got it, and haven't started yet |
| **In progress** | Someone is actively working on it |
| **Resolved** | We believe it's sorted |
| **Closed** | The case is finished and no longer open for replies |
| **Dismissed** | No action was needed — often a duplicate |

You'll get a notification and an email whenever we reply, so you don't need to keep checking.

<Note>
  You can reply in the thread while a case is open. Once it moves to **Closed** or **Dismissed**, the reply box is replaced with "This case is closed." That's expected, not a bug — if the problem comes back, open a new request and mention the old case number.
</Note>

---

## Response times

Expect a reply within a few business days for most questions.

There's no phone line and no live chat — the three options above are the way in.
