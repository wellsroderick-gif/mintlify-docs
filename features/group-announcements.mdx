WR-Games gives your group six ways to communicate.

| Feature | What It Does |
| :-- | :-- |
| Announcements | One message to every member |
| Welcome Message | An email to new members |
| Group Chat | Live conversation, members only |
| Forum | Organized threads that stay findable |
| Group Polls | A question with set answers |
| Schedule Finder | Members vote on times |

<Info>
  Group Chat, the Forum, Group Polls, and Schedule Finder are apps. Organizers turn each one on or off from the gear icon on the group page, under **Features → Apps**. When an app is off, its tab is hidden on the group page for everyone.
</Info>

## Announcements

Announcements send one message to every member of your group. Members get it in the app and, unless they've turned it off, by email.

### Send an Announcement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Group">
    On the group page, find the **Send Announcement** button on the group bar. It only appears for members who are allowed to send announcements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write It">
    Add a **Title** (it becomes the notification title and the email subject), then write your **Message**. The message box supports formatting. On a phone, the form opens from the bottom of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send It">
    Select **Send**, then **Send now** to confirm. You'll see **Announcement on its way**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Announcements go out shortly after you confirm, not the instant you select **Send now**.

### Who Gets It

Everyone in the group, including you.

| Where It Shows Up | Who Gets It |
| :-- | :-- |
| In-app notification | Every member |
| Messages inbox | Every member |
| Email | Members who haven't turned off announcement emails for this group |

<Note>
  Each group can send 3 announcements in any 24 hours. The limit belongs to the group, so everyone who can send shares it. The Send Announcement form shows how many are used ("N of 3 used in the last 24 hours"). At 3, **Send** is turned off until the oldest one is more than 24 hours old.
</Note>

### Email Preferences

Each member chooses their own email settings for a group. Select the gear icon on the group page, then **My Preferences**. The **Email Preferences** card has two separate switches:

- **Receive announcement emails from this group**
- **Receive new event emails from this group**

Turning a switch off stops only the email. In-app notifications always arrive. Every announcement email also ends with a **Manage email preferences** link.

<Tip>
  Announcements reach everyone, so save them for news people shouldn't miss.
</Tip>

## Welcome Message

The welcome message is an email, not something shown on the group page. It goes out when someone joins right away, or when you approve their request to join. It's off until you turn it on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Card">
    Select the gear icon on the group page, then **Profile**, and open the **Welcome Message** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn It On and Write It">
    Switch on **Send welcome message to new members**, write your message in **Message Body**, and save. The message box supports formatting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Members you approve get two emails: the approval email and your welcome message. New members also see the welcome message as a notification and in their Messages inbox.

## Group Chat

Group Chat is real-time chat for your group's members. You'll find it on the group's **Chat** tab.

In chat you can:

- Reply to a specific message
- React with an emoji
- Post links, which become clickable automatically

The Chat tab shows a badge when there are messages you haven't read. People who moderate the group can remove messages.

## Forum

The forum holds organized threads that stay easy to find. It's divided into **sections**, and each section shows who can see it: **Members Only**, **Organizers Only**, or **Restricted**.

Each thread has a type: **Discussion**, **LFG**, **Game Review**, **Recap**, or **Announcement**. Replies to a thread appear in order, one after another, rather than nested.

People who moderate the group can **pin** a thread to keep it visible and **lock** it so it stops taking new replies.

<Note>
  A lock also stops members editing the thread and their own replies on it. People who moderate the group can still edit, delete, pin and unlock.
</Note>

You can also attach a poll to a thread when you start it (see Group Polls below).

## Group Polls

Group Polls put a question to your group and collect votes. They live on the group's **Polls** tab, and you can attach one to a forum thread.

Whoever creates the poll chooses these settings:

| Setting | What It Does |
| :-- | :-- |
| Question | What you're asking the group |
| Description | Optional extra detail |
| Poll Type | **Single Choice** or **Multiple Choice** |
| Options | 2 to 10 |
| Allow Vote Change | Off by default. When on, members can change or remove their vote |
| Show Results Before Voting | Off by default. When on, counts show before someone votes |
| Close Date | Optional |

## Schedule Finder

Schedule Finder helps you pick a time. You offer a set of time slots, members mark which ones work, and you choose a winner. Polls are visible to group members only, on the group's **Schedule Finder** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the Poll">
    On the Schedule Finder tab, select **Create a Poll** and give it a title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Who Can Vote">
    Pick **Everyone in the Group**, or **Specific Members (Invite-Only)** and select the members. Invite-only polls are visible only to the people you invite and to you. You can add more invitees later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Your Slots">
    Choose a slot length (30 min, 1 hr, 90 min, 2 hr, or a custom length), then click time slots on the weekly grid. Slots use the group's time zone unless you change it. You can add an optional deadline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Members vote by tapping a slot, which cycles through **Yes**, **Maybe**, and **No**. Polls are listed under **Open** and **Closed**. When it's time to decide, you can close the poll, pick the winning slot, and start a new event with that date and time already filled in.

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A member says they didn't get the announcement email">
    They may have turned announcement emails off for this group. They still got the in-app notification, and the announcement is in their Messages inbox. They can turn emails back on in **Email Preferences**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Send is turned off">
    Your group has used all 3 announcements for the last 24 hours. You can send another once the oldest one is more than 24 hours old.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did I get my own announcement?">
    Announcements go to everyone in the group, including the person who sends them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
