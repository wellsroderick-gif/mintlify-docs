Closing for renovations, a long holiday, or for good? Tell WR-Games, and members and hosts will know before they show up at a locked door.

Both kinds of closure live in **Settings** in Manage. If you haven't been in Manage before, start with [Run Your Venue](/features/running-your-venue).

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Temporary Closure" icon="calendar-x">
    Closed for a set time, or until you're ready to reopen. Your venue stays in the directory with a closed banner.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Permanently Close" icon="door-closed">
    Closed for good. Your venue leaves the directory, but its page and events stay. You can still reopen later.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
  Closed for just a day or two, like a holiday? Use [Special Hours](/features/running-your-venue) on the Hours tab instead.
</Tip>

## Who Can Close the Venue

| Closure | Who Can Set It |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Temporary Closure** | The owner, and anyone on the team whose role includes Temporary Closure. The Manager role has it to start. |
| **Permanently Close** | The owner only |

[Run Your Venue With a Team](/features/run-your-venue-with-a-team) covers roles.

## Closing Temporarily

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In Manage, open **Settings**. Under **Temporary Closure**, select **Set Closure**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Your Dates">
    Choose **Closed From**, then **Reopens On**. Not sure when you'll reopen? Tick **Until I Reopen** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Note (Optional)">
    Tell members why, like "Closed for renovations."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Set Closure**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your note shows publicly on your venue page. Don't put anything in it you wouldn't want every member to read.
</Note>

Once it's set, the Temporary Closure row shows your dates. If you picked a reopen date, your venue reopens by itself on that day.

## Closing for Good

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Danger Zone">
    In Manage, open **Settings**. In the **Danger Zone**, find **Permanently Close** and select **Close Venue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Note (Optional)">
    This note shows publicly on your venue page too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select **Close Venue** again on **Close This Venue for Good?**
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your venue leaves the directory, so new members won't find it there. Its page stays up, and so do the events already linked to it.

<Note>
  Want to take the venue off WR-Games altogether instead? Use **Request Removal**, also in the Danger Zone. [Run Your Venue With a Team](/features/run-your-venue-with-a-team) covers how that works.
</Note>

## Reopening

Select **Reopen Venue** on whichever closure you set. This works for both kinds, so closing for good isn't the end if your plans change.

A venue that's permanently closed has to be reopened before you can set a temporary closure on it.

## What Hosts See

Hosts plan events at your venue, so a closure tells them.

- **A notice** goes to the hosts of upcoming events at your venue when you close it.
- **A warning on their event page:** a host whose event falls during your closure sees **This Venue Is Temporarily Closed** or **This Venue Has Closed** on the event, with your dates. Only hosts and organizers see it, not attendees.

The warning offers **Change Venue**, which opens the event to pick somewhere else, and **Dismiss**. Dismiss hides it for that visit only. It comes back until the host moves the event or cancels it.

Closing your venue never cancels anyone's event. Each host decides what to do with theirs.
