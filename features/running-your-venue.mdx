Once your venue is yours, **Manage** is where you run it. You can change its details, hours, location and photos yourself, and your changes go live as soon as you save. There's no staff review.

Not running a venue yet? Start with [Claim Your Venue](/features/claiming-your-venue).

## Getting to Manage

There are two ways in:

- **From your venue's page:** select **Manage Venue** at the top. Only the venue's owner and team see it.
- **From My Venues:** open **My Venues** from your account menu. Every venue you run is under **Venues You Run**, with **Manage** next to it.

Team members see the tabs their role allows. Everything below assumes you're the owner, who sees them all.

## The Tabs

| Tab | What You Do There |
| :-- | :-- |
| **General** | A snapshot of your venue: anything that needs you, upcoming events, your team and how your listing looks |
| **Profile** | Name, description, type, contact details, amenities, capacity, menu link and accessibility |
| **Hours** | Your regular week, an hours note and special hours for one-off dates |
| **Location** | Your address, map pin and How to Find Us |
| **Images** | Your cover photo and header image |
| **Calendar Feed** | Bring in events from your own calendar, and share a subscribe link. See [Venue Calendar Feed](/features/venue-calendar-feed). |
| **Events** | Your partners and the labels on events at your venue |
| **Team** | Who helps run the venue, and what each role can do |
| **Settings** | Closures, handing the venue to someone else and requesting removal |

A few more tabs, like Reservations, Tables and Reviews, show **Coming Soon**. They don't do anything yet.

<Tip>
  Each tab saves on its own, from the bar at the bottom of the tab. Save before you switch tabs.
</Tip>

## General: Your Snapshot

General is the first thing you see when you open Manage.

- **Needs Attention** lists anything waiting on you, like reports about your venue. When it's clear, it says **Nothing needs you right now.**
- **Upcoming Events** and **Team** show how many of each you have.
- **Events Here** breaks down the next 30 days of events at your venue by label: Official, Partner and Community-Led.
- **Your Listing** shows how your venue appears in the directory.

## Profile: Your Venue's Details

Profile holds everything a member reads about your venue: its name, description, type, contact details and amenities.

Renaming your venue doesn't change its web address, so links people have already shared keep working.

### Amenities and Public Restroom

Amenities like Wi-Fi, Sells Games and Outdoor Space can be set to **Yes**, **No** or **Not Set**. Members can filter the venue directory by them, and a filter only shows venues that said **Yes**.

**Public Restroom** means anyone can use your restroom, not just paying customers. It has its own directory filter, separate from accessibility.

### Capacity, Menu and Accessibility

These are optional. Anything you leave empty stays off your venue page.

| Field | What It Does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Capacity** | How many people your venue holds. If a host's event allows more people than this, they see a note. It's only a heads-up and never stops the event. |
| **Menu Link** | A link to your food or drink menu |
| **Step-Free Entrance**, **Accessible Restroom**, **Accessible Seating**, **Accessible Parking**, **Elevator** | Each is **Yes**, **No** or **Not Set**. A **No** shows on your venue page as No. **Not Set** stays off the page. |

Members can filter the directory by each accessibility detail. Like the amenity filters, each one only shows venues that said **Yes**.

## Hours: Your Week and Special Dates

Set your regular hours for each day of the week, and add an hours note if there's something members should know.

### Special Hours

Use **Special Hours** for dates when your hours differ from your regular week, like a holiday closure or a short day.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Date">
    In the **Special Hours** card, select **Add Special Hours** and pick the date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Hours">
    Tick **Closed** if you're closed all day. Otherwise, choose when you open and close. A closing time earlier than the opening time runs past midnight, the same as your weekly hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Note (Optional)">
    Tell members why, like "Closed for the holiday."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Your weekly hours and special hours save together.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On your venue page, members see an **Upcoming Changes** section inside your Hours card. It lists any special dates in the next 14 days, and past dates drop off on their own.

<Note>
  Closing for a week or more? Manage suggests a **Temporary Closure** instead. You'll find it in Settings. Your special hours still save if you'd rather keep them.
</Note>

## Location: Address, Map Pin and Directions

### Changing Your Address

Upcoming events at your venue move to the new address automatically, and their hosts get a notice when you save. Past events keep the address they happened at.

### Moving the Map Pin

If the map puts your pin in the wrong spot, drag it to your entrance and save. Once you've placed the pin yourself, your venue page map and **Get Directions** use the pin instead of the address.

Two questions can come up when you save:

| You See | When | Your Choices |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Save the Pin This Far Away?** | The pin is a long way from your address | **Move the Pin** to fix it, or **Save the Pin** if it's right |
| **Move the Pin to the New Address?** | You placed the pin by hand, then changed the address | **Reset the Pin** to the new address, or **Keep the Pin** where it is |

### How to Find Us

A short note that helps people get in the door: which entrance, which floor, where to park. It shows under your address on your venue page, in the **Getting There** card, and in the location details of every upcoming event at your venue.

Changing only How to Find Us doesn't notify anyone.

## Images: Cover Photo and Header Image

| Image | Where It Shows |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Cover Photo** | Your venue card in the directory, and your venue page |
| **Header Image** | A wide banner across the top of your venue page. If you don't set one, your cover photo is used. |

Both take JPEG, PNG or WebP.

## What Hosts Hear About Your Changes

Hosts plan events around your venue, so some changes tell them.

| You Change | Hosts of Upcoming Events Here |
| :-- | :-- |
| Your address or map pin | Get a notice |
| Your hours or special hours | Get a notice only if their event now falls outside your hours |
| How to Find Us | Aren't notified |
| Anything else | Aren't notified |

## Your Venue in My Venues

**My Venues**, in your account menu, lists every venue you run under **Venues You Run**, whether you own it or are on its team. **Manage** takes you straight in. [Claim Your Venue](/features/claiming-your-venue) covers the rest of the page: your claims and the venues you've submitted.
