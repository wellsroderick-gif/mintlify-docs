If your game collection already lives on BGG, there is no need to rebuild it from scratch on WR-Games. Connecting your BGG account pulls in your collections automatically and organises them in your WR-Games collection.

<Note>
  WR-Games only reads your BGG collection data. It never writes back to BGG or modifies your BGG profile, ratings, or collections in any way.
</Note>

## How to Connect Your BGG Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your BGG Username">
    Type your username into the **BGG Username** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Which Collections to Import">
    WR-Games shows a list of your BGG collection categories. **Owned** and **Want to Play** are checked by default. These are the most useful for finding and attending local events. Toggle any of the other collections on or off to match what you care about.

    <Note>
      WR-Games plans to use your collection data to power more features over time: for example, games in your **Want to Buy** or **Preordered** collections may be used to surface alerts and recommendations in the future.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Sync">
    Click **Start sync**. WR-Games begins fetching your collection from BGG.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the Sync to Complete">
    The sync typically finishes in under a minute. You will see a confirmation once your games are ready in your collection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

No games on WR-Games yet? You can also connect from the [game picker](/features/game-picker). Select **Sync From BoardGameGeek**, enter your username, and keep picking while your collection comes in.

<Tip>
  Use the **Select All** and **Clear All** buttons above the category list to configure your collections quickly without toggling each one individually.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Want to Play** games import into their own folder. When you open your Collection page, it defaults to showing your **Owned** games. Switch to the Want to Play tab to see that collection.
</Note>

## What Gets Synced

WR-Games reads up to 7 BGG collection categories. You choose which ones to include:

| Collection | What It Includes |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Want to Play** | Games on your BGG Want to Play list |
| **Owned** | Games you have marked as owned |
| **Wishlist** | Games you want to acquire |
| **For Trade** | Games you are willing to trade away |
| **Want to Buy** | Games you are actively looking to purchase |
| **Preordered** | Games you have on preorder |
| **Previously Owned** | Games you owned in the past |

<Note>
  Event alerts come from your **WR-Games Favorites**, not from your BGG collections. Favorite a game and you'll hear when it's coming up at an event near you. See [Favorite Game Alerts](/help/notifications).
</Note>

## After Syncing

Once the sync completes, your games appear in your **Collection** tab, organised by the collection categories you enabled. From there you can browse by collection, search within your collection, and view each game's detail page.

## Keeping Your Collection Up to Date

**Auto-sync with BGG** is a checkbox on the BGG card in **Settings → Integrations**. It starts checked, and your choice is saved when you sync. With it on, WR-Games refreshes your collection from BGG on its own in the background, so a game you add on BGG turns up here without you lifting a finger. Background refreshes don't send you a notification.

Want a change to show up right now? After your first sync, the card shows **Save & Re-Sync**. Use it to add or remove collections, or to pull in your latest BGG changes straight away.

### If Auto-Sync Stops

When auto-sync isn't running, a line under the checkbox tells you why.

| What You See | What to Do |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto-sync is off.** | You unchecked the box. Check it to turn auto-sync back on. |
| **Auto-sync is paused because you haven't visited in a while.** | Check the box, or sync, and it starts again. |
| **Auto-sync is off because we couldn't find your BGG username.** | Check the username for typos, then sync. |
| **Auto-sync is off because BGG didn't respond for a week.** | BGG was having trouble on its end. Check the box or sync to try again. |

If auto-sync turns itself off, you get one notification so it doesn't go quiet without you knowing.

## Resetting or Wiping Your Collection

The **Danger Zone** in **Settings → Integrations** gives you two options for clearing your synced data. Both are destructive, but with very different blast radii. Read before you act.

|  | Reset BGG Sync | Wipe Collection |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| BGG-synced games | Removed | Removed |
| Sync history & status | Cleared | Cleared |
| Manual games | Kept | Removed |
| Custom folders | Kept | Removed |
| Favorites | Kept | Kept |
| BGG username | Kept | Cleared |
| Board Game Arena (BGA) username | Kept | Kept |
| Auto-contribute setting | Kept on | Cleared |
| Recovery | Re-sync anytime | Reconnect from scratch |

<Warning>
  **Reset BGG sync** clears your synced library and sync history, but nothing you've built manually is touched. Your manual games, custom folders, Favorites, BGG username, and any games you've contributed to groups by hand all stay. Auto-contribute stays on, so the next sync rebuilds your collection based on what you currently own.

  Use Reset when your sync is in a bad state and you want a clean re-pull, or to clear out games you no longer own.
</Warning>

<Danger>
  **Wipe collection** goes further. It removes everything Reset removes, plus your manual games and custom folders, and it clears your BGG username and disconnects the integration entirely. Your favorites stay. This cannot be undone. Reconnecting and re-syncing from scratch is the only way back.

  Use Wipe only if you want to completely cut ties with your current BGG link and start over from zero.
</Danger>

<Note>
  Neither option affects your BGG account or your Board Game Arena (BGA) username. Everything stays exactly as it is on those platforms.
</Note>

If you've also imported plays from BG Stats, those have their own Reset and Wipe pair in the same Danger zone. The two sets are independent: clearing your BG Stats data leaves your collection alone, and the two options above leave your imported plays alone. See [BG Stats Import](/features/bg-stats) for what each one clears.
