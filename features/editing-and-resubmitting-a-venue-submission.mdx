After you submit a venue, we review it before it's confirmed. Until then it shows **Pending Verification**. You can still change it while you wait, and if it isn't approved, you get a chance to fix it and send it back.

## While Your Venue Is Pending

A venue that's waiting for review shows **Pending Verification** on its card and on its page. On your own venue's page, a banner reads **Your submission is pending verification**, with an **Edit Submission** button. Nobody else sees that banner.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Venue's Page">
    Go to the venue you submitted. If you're coming from a reminder notification, its link takes you straight to the edit form, so you can skip ahead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Edit Submission">
    The **Edit Your Venue Submission** form opens with your answers already filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Your Changes and Resubmit">
    Change what you need and resubmit. Your venue goes back for review and shows **Pending Verification** again. We'll let you know when it's reviewed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If Your Submission Isn't Approved

You get a notice, and your venue's page shows a banner that reads **Your submission was not approved**. It gives the reason and the date to fix it by. Until then, only you can see the venue.

| What the banner shows | What it means | What you can do |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Edit and resubmit it by** a date | You have 14 days from the rejection to fix it. | Select **Edit Submission**, make your changes, and resubmit. |
| A reason and a removal date, with no **Edit Submission** button | This one can't be resubmitted. | Read the reason. The submission is removed on the date shown. |

Three days before the date, you get a reminder: **Your venue submission will be removed soon**. It links to the edit form, so you can fix it and resubmit from there.

<Warning>
  When the date passes, the submission is removed and you get a notice. You can submit the venue again whenever you like.
</Warning>

When you resubmit, the rejection is cleared and the venue goes back to **Pending Verification**.

## Yes, No, or Not Set

The Amenities step has four questions you can answer **Yes**, **No**, or **Not Set**: **Wi-Fi**, **Accessible**, **Sells Games**, and **Outdoor Space**. **Not Set** is the starting answer, and it isn't the same as **No**. It means nobody has said either way.

<Note>
  When someone filters the venue directory by one of these amenities, only a **Yes** matches. A venue left on **Not Set** doesn't show up, so it's worth answering the ones you know.
</Note>
