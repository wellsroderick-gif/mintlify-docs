If email to your account address keeps bouncing back, WR-Games stops sending to it. Event reminders, RSVP updates and every other email stop until it's sorted out. You'll know because of two things:

- A notification in your bell: **We Can't Reach Your Email**. Select it to go to your Account Settings.
- An amber box under **Email Address** in your Account Settings: "We can't deliver email to" your address, with the note that messages bounced and sending has stopped.

Your notifications in the bell keep arriving the whole time. Only email is paused.

## Fixing It

Check the address shown in the box.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="The Address Is Right">
    Select **This Address Is Correct**. Email starts again from the next one WR-Games sends. Nothing is sent right away, so don't wait for a test message.

    If it bounces again, the warning comes back. That usually means your email provider is turning our messages away. Check with your provider, then try again.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="The Address Is Wrong">
    Email [support@wr-games.com](mailto:support@wr-games.com) and tell us the right address. You can also use [Contact us](/help/contact).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  This warning only shows up when email bounces. If you've stopped getting WR-Games email and don't see it, check your spam folder and your [notification settings](/help/notifications), then [contact us](/help/contact) if it's still missing.
</Note>
